## Context

`EPIC-07`'s "Done when" asks for the bar **reported on the held-out set**, "the
number v1 never had", and its item 4 says the story that meets the bar "earns
the epic's first held-out score". The bar is **containment plus absence**: a
crop contains the marked rectangle and contains no browser, OS or reader
furniture (`product-brief.md` §5, third amendment of 2026-09-21). The marks are
the never-clip oracle, not an accuracy target, and nobody re-marks.

[MC-050](MC-050.md) found that no new furniture rule is needed **on the tuning
set**. MC-048's crop alone has 0 clips on 21 of 21 and no reader furniture on
19 of 21 (`reader-furniture-search.md` §1). It also found that this does not
show that a reader-agnostic rule exists (§5). Its §6 recommends this story: rule
the reader furniture on the marked held-out entries first, with the ruling page
drawing neither the app's crop nor the marks, then run the crop over them once.
This is the first time any rule meets `Split::HeldOut`, and `corpus.md` says
the story that does so "selects `Split::HeldOut` deliberately, once, and says
so in its own file". This is that file.

**What is being spent.** Counted from `fixtures/corpus/manifest.json` at
`32632d4`, by count only, with no file named:

- **24 marked held-out entries**: `rolia-scans` 7, `toongod` 7, `xbato` 3,
  `kunmanga` 2, `manhwaclan` 2, `w-network` 2, `demonicrevolution` 1.
  `xbato` and `manhwaclan` are the two **unseen** readers (`corpus.md`,
  `PRE_EPIC_07_SITES`). None of the 24 is a WebP, so MC-048's named WebP
  limitation does not bear on them directly.
- **7 flag held-out entries** (`"expect": "flag"`): `toongod` 3, `w-network` 3,
  `rolia-scans` 1.

`corpus.md`'s rule governs every step: the set is scored **once** and never
tuned against. No threshold is chosen, adjusted or rejected on a held-out
result, and nobody reads a per-file held-out result before the rule that
produced it is frozen. An entry used to make such a decision is no longer
held out, and moving it to `tuning` is irreversible. The `integration` gate
never spends held-out, so this run is **not** added to it. It lives in a
harness outside the repository, as MC-031's and MC-050's did.

**Prior contact with held-out, and why it is not contamination.** In MC-048's
RED a scratch binary printed the page column, the mark rows and the oracle rows
for the marked held-out entries. Nothing was scored, written down or used, and
the user ruled on 2026-09-23 that the entries **stay held out** (MC-048
`## Amendments`, Open question 2). If MC-049 runs its own aggregate held-out
clip count (its Open question 4), that is an aggregate fact and not a per-file
result. But if it reports a clip, that entry leaves held-out, and the counts
above shrink. See Open question 1.

**Flag entries: decided at planning, not a bar.** The 7 flag entries are in the
run, and their outcomes are reported as counts. **No pass or fail rides on
them.** The reasons:

- `EPIC-07`'s predicate is defined against a mark, and a flag entry has none.
- MC-026 AC-4 treats flags as a **control pinned per file** on tuning (six
  pinned, `2025-02-27 22_46_15.png` the accepted baseline), not as a rate.
- The only time flags entered a 9-in-10 was MC-019 AC-2, which is the
  column-axis window `EPIC-07` does not use.

Inventing a flag rate here would be inventing a bar. A cropped flag entry has
no mark, so it cannot be a clip.

**A marked entry answered `Flagged`** is copied unchanged. Its output is the
whole image: it contains the mark, and it is scored for absence against the
whole image, so it normally fails absence. MC-019 AC-2 also counted a flagged
marked entry as a miss.

**The required gate.** This story commits no code. `integration` is the gate
that would fail if the scored tree's tuning-side claims broke, and it is
optional, hence `required_gates` (MC-038's reasoning, MC-039's binding).

## Definitions used by every criterion

- **Scored commit**: the `main` commit whose release build is run. It is
  recorded in the document, with the harness's source hash, **before** AC-3
  runs.
- **The crop**: `process_file`'s outcome at `Tuning::default()` on the scored
  commit, release build. For `Cropped { rect }`, the rows are
  `rect.y .. rect.y + rect.h` (bottom exclusive). For `Flagged`, the whole
  image.
- **Clip**: the crop does not contain the marked rect on all four sides.
  Columns count too, because MC-049 moves them.
- **Top furniture end `T`**: the first row below *all* furniture ruled at the
  top (browser chrome and reader header, per Open question 4), or 0 when none
  is ruled. **Bottom furniture start `B`**: the first row of any furniture
  ruled at the bottom (reader footer and taskbar), or the image height when
  none is ruled.
- **Furniture-free**: `crop.y >= T` and `crop.y + crop.h <= B`. The comparison
  is exact to the row, as MC-050's was. A one-row remnant is present.
- **Meets the bar**: no clip and furniture-free. The bar is **0 clips on 24 of
  24** (absolute, `EPIC-07`) **and at least 22 of 24** meeting it. 22 is the
  brief's 9 in 10 of 24 (21.6) rounded up, which is the same arithmetic that
  gave MC-050's 19 of 21.

## Acceptance criteria

The denominators are **24** (marked held-out) and **7** (flag held-out) as
counted in `## Context`. If an entry has left held-out before this story starts
(Open question 1), AC-1 records the new counts per reader, and every AC below
reads them in place of 24 and 7. Nothing is re-counted after AC-3 runs.

- **AC-1** — *The held-out furniture oracle, ruled blind.* Given the 24 marked
  held-out entries, when the user rules them through an Artifact page, then the
  document records for each entry:
  - the reader header's end and the reader footer's start. This uses MC-050's
    round-2 wording: the site's own full-row bars only, page gutter stays, and
    an overlay drawn on the art is not furniture.
  - per Open question 4, the browser chrome's end and the taskbar's start as
    separate rows.

  Each row may be "none visible", and each names who ruled it and when. The
  page shows the image and full-resolution top and bottom bands. It draws
  **none** of the following:
  - the app's crop;
  - the viewport MC-048's stage locates;
  - any row computed by `cropper-core` or `cropper-engine`;
  - any proposed row;
  - the manifest marks.

  Every line on the page is labelled by origin, and the only lines are the
  user's own placements. The rulings are stored in the Artifact's database and
  are read back into the document table with the database tool, not typed.
  They are **frozen by a dated line in `## Notes` before AC-3 runs**. No
  ruling changes afterwards. The harness step that builds the page's data
  emits only file name, reader, image size and image previews. The document
  shows that output's key list as the mechanical check that no crop or mark
  reached the page.
- **AC-2** — *Before any crop touches held-out: ceiling, harness check,
  controls.* Three parts, all run before AC-3, each with its output pasted:
  - (a) **Oracle against mark.** Count the marked held-out entries whose `T`
    lies below the mark's top or whose `B` lies above the mark's bottom. On
    those entries, containment and absence cannot both hold (MC-050 §3's
    `Screenshot (67).png` case). This is computed from the rulings and the
    marks only, with no crop. The ceiling is 24 minus that count. If it is not
    0, those entries are put to the user by name before AC-3. That is oracle
    data, not a result.
  - (b) **Tuning reproduction.** The same harness scoring code, pointed at the
    21 marked tuning entries with MC-050's round-2 rulings (reader furniture
    only, as ruled there), reproduces `reader-furniture-search.md` §1 at the
    scored commit: **0 clips on 21 of 21, reader-furniture-free on 19 of 21**.
    Any difference is explained in `## Notes`, from the scored commit's own
    story (e.g. MC-049's margin change), before held-out is touched.
  - (c) **Controls on held-out oracle data only, no crop run.** First, a
    "crop" equal to the whole image must report furniture present on
    **exactly** the entries whose rulings name any furniture. That count is
    known from AC-1, and a check that reports fewer is blind. Second, a "crop"
    equal to each mark with 10 rows removed from its top and its bottom must
    report a clip on **24 of 24**. Both are shown firing.
- **AC-3** — *One run.* Given the frozen rulings and the scored commit, when
  the harness runs the crop **once** over the 31 held-out entries, then the
  document reports these as counts:
  - **clips**: *n* of 24. Any clip is a stop-and-ask (Open question 3). It is
    put to the user before anything else in this list is acted on.
  - **meets the bar**: *n* of 24, against 22. Furniture-free is also broken
    down by kind: reader furniture absent *n* of 24, browser and OS furniture
    absent *n* of 24.
  - **viewport**: MC-048's stage located *n* / declined *n*, over the 24 and
    over the 7 separately. This is read from the stage directly, as
    `corpus_viewport_stage` does.
  - **per reader**, for all seven readers: entries, zero-clip,
    furniture-free, meets the bar, viewport located. `xbato` (3) and
    `manhwaclan` (2) are marked unseen and reported **as a direction, not a
    percentage** (`corpus.md`).
  - **flags**: of the 7, `Flagged` *n* and `Cropped` *n*. There is no bar
    (`## Context`).
  - **marked entries answered `Flagged`**: *n* of 24, scored as defined above.

  Per-file lines appear only as Open question 2 allows. The harness refuses a
  second held-out run once its output file exists. The document records the
  command, the UTC time, the scored commit and the harness source hash, and
  quotes the aggregate block verbatim.
- **AC-4** — *The verdict and what it forces.* Exactly one of:
  - (a) **Met**: 0 clips on 24 of 24 and at least 22 of 24 meet the bar.
    `EPIC-07` item 4 is **earned on held-out**, and `EPIC-07` says so with the
    number, the date and the scored commit.
  - (b) **Not met, 0 clips**: the failing entries by cause (reader header,
    reader footer, browser chrome, taskbar, flagged), per Open question 2. The
    next story is named, most likely MC-050 §6's same-tone-header rule, fitted
    on tuning plus the now-read failing entries. Held-out owed: `EPIC-07`'s
    held-out score then has to come from **new** screenshots.
  - (c) **A clip**: the verdict is a failure of the absolute half, whatever the
    furniture count. The handling is Open question 3's answer: a never-clip
    `fix` story is recommended, and the entry moves to `tuning`.

  In every case the document states **what has been spent**:

  - the 24 marked and 7 flag entries have given their one score at the scored
    commit;
  - any later run over them is a **re-score on spent held-out**, is labelled
    that way wherever it is reported, and is **never** `EPIC-07`'s held-out
    score;
  - every entry read per file is `tuning` for any later rule;
  - a fresh held-out score needs new screenshots from the user (a story shaped
    like MC-037).

  Moving entries to `tuning` in the manifest is **recommended as a follow-up
  chore, not done here** (`## Out of scope`).
- **AC-5** — *The record.* The document is `docs/wiki/held-out-score.md`, and
  the following files change with it:
  - `corpus.md`'s "tuning / held-out split" section gains a dated paragraph
    recording the spend: scored commit, date, counts, and which entries were
    read per file.
  - `EPIC-07` item 4 and "Done when" carry the verdict.
  - `v2-candidates.md` gains a row.

  Every number is reproducible from a harness outside the repository whose
  path the document records. The harness drops every entry whose `split` is
  not `held-out` **before a file is named or decoded**, and asserts 24 marked
  and 7 flag. The tuning path of AC-2b is a separate, named function.

variable, not the model. The orchestrator (Lead PO) runs this story itself, as

  - the 9-in-10 bar and its 22-of-24 arithmetic;
  - zero clips as absolute;
  - `corpus.md`'s held-out rule, word for word;

  - MC-050's round-2 tuning rulings and §1's numbers (AC-2b's target);
  - the flag decision in `## Context`;

- **A person's call, never an agent's.** Every held-out ruling in AC-1, any
  oracle-against-mark conflict AC-2a finds, and what happens on a clip. The
  agent does not view held-out images: it generates previews and uploads them,
  and the user looks.
- **Mechanical, so pin it exactly.**
  - the denominators (24, 7, per reader);
  - the definitions above;
  - the harness's held-out filter and its assertions;
  - the run-once guard;
  - the reporting shape of AC-3;
  - AC-5's edits.
- **Oracle-free**: none. The ruling page's layout is the only design latitude,
  and AC-1 fixes what it may not show.

**Success condition, which can come out either way.** A reader of
`held-out-score.md` can say, with one number and its denominator, whether
`EPIC-07`'s bar is met on held-out, and can trust that the number was produced
blind. It **fails** if any of the following happens:

- the ruling page drew a crop, a viewport, a proposal or a mark;
- a ruling changed after AC-3 ran;
- AC-2 ran after AC-3;
- the harness changed after held-out output was seen;
- held-out was run twice;
- a per-file held-out result was read outside Open question 2's answer.

**Record the verdict in `## Notes`.**

**Timebox.** One ruling sitting for the user, about 20 minutes for 24 entries
at four rows each, then one run.

## Open questions — answer before it starts

**Answered 2026-09-24: the user (ryanczhang7) accepted the recommended default
on all four, before it started.** In their words, as put to them:

1. Test after MC-049 is done, so that the one run measures the finished crop
   (`depends_on: [MC-049, MC-050]` stands).
2. Totals plus failures: aggregate and per-reader counts, with a per-file look
   only at the failing entries.
3. On a clip, stop and ask.
4. Mark all four lines per screenshot (browser chrome end, reader header end,
   reader footer start, taskbar start).

The corollary to 1 also stands: the MC-049 session should not run its own
aggregate held-out clip count, so that held-out is touched only by this story.

1. **Sequencing against MC-049. This is the most important question in the
   story.** Held-out is scored **once**. MC-049, being worked in another
   session, drops `margin_px` to 0 and moves the side edges. That is exactly
   the change most likely to clip an entry no one has tuned against, and
   containment is half the bar. If this story scores MC-048's crop now, v1's
   crop *with* MC-049 never gets a held-out score, because the entries are
   spent.
   - *Recommended:* `depends_on: [MC-049]`, as the frontmatter already says,
     so that the one run judges the crop v1 actually ships.
   - *Corollary:* MC-049's own Open question 4 (an aggregate held-out clip
     count at the end of its GATES) is then a second run of the same crop over
     the same entries. **Recommend telling that session to skip it**, so that
     held-out is touched once, by this story.
   - *Caveat:* "the crop v1 ships" is final only against the stories known
     today. `EPIC-08` item 2 (conditional) or a same-tone-header rule would
     change the crop again, and would need new held-out screenshots.
   - *Alternative:* score now, on MC-048's crop, with `depends_on: [MC-050]`.
     You accept that MC-049's crop is never scored on held-out except by its
     own aggregate clip count.
2. **May per-file held-out results be read after the run?** `corpus.md` allows
   it once the rule is frozen, but any entry read per file is contaminated for
   every later rule.
   - *Recommended:* aggregate counts and per-reader counts, plus a per-file
     table **only for failing entries**. A failing entry is one that clips, or
     is not furniture-free, or is a marked entry answered `Flagged`. For each,
     the table gives the cause and the rows. Those entries become `tuning` for
     any later rule; passing entries stay unread.
   - *Alternatives:* aggregate only, which keeps all 24 unread but leaves a
     miss undiagnosable; or a full per-file table, which makes all 24 tuning
     for any later rule.
3. **What happens on a clip?**
   - *Recommended:* **stop and ask**, before the rest of AC-3 is acted on.
   - The clipped entry is named, by necessity, and moves to `tuning`
     permanently (`corpus.md`), through a follow-up chore.
   - The verdict is a failure of the absolute half, and a never-clip `fix`
     story is filed.
   - **Nothing is re-scored**: neither the other entries nor this one on a
     fixed crop.
4. **Does the ruling page rule browser chrome and taskbar too, or only reader
   furniture?** `EPIC-07`'s predicate names all three kinds. MC-050's round-2
   wording assigned browser and taskbar to MC-048 because MC-050 asked only
   about the reader. On held-out there is no independent chrome oracle:
   MC-031 §4's rows are tuning-only, and MC-048's own located viewport cannot
   judge MC-048's crop.
   - *Recommended:* four rows per entry: browser chrome end, reader header
     end, reader footer start, taskbar start. Each has "none visible" and is
     labelled by origin on the page, and the reader rows keep the round-2
     wording verbatim. The cost is two more clicks per entry.
   - *Alternative:* reader rows only. A marked entry whose viewport MC-048
     declined then counts as **not** furniture-free, and a located viewport
     counts as browser- and OS-free on trust. That trust is unverified on
     held-out.
