three are on the **column axis**. A fourth entry failed with no clip, and

Everything below is inferred from rectangles alone.
- **`f09` and `f20` look like one kind of bug**: the right edge stops a few
  columns short of the art, and the left edge is exact.
  - `f20` is one of the four the user ticked "diagonal" while marking. The
    user ruled that the diagonal lies between panels and moves no edge.
  - Whether the right-edge art is low-contrast, overhanging, or something
    else is unknown.
- **`f18` and `f13` look like another kind.**
  - Both marks sit on the **left half** of the screen (columns 635..1168),
    where a reader window takes half a split screen. MC-052's two
    split-screen screenshots, `2025-03-06 01_22_45.png` and
    `2025-03-07 00_58_06.png`, are from the same reader and days.
  - `f18`'s crop runs the full height on the right, which is where a second
    window would be. `f13`'s crop runs from the mark's left edge nearly to
    the screen's right edge.
  - A second browser window is a **candidate explanation, not a finding**.
    [MC-052](MC-052.md) taught the viewport stage to read the reader's own
    window on its two split-screen shots. That did not carry to these.

  which one was not looked up. If it is one of these four, RED will see it.

**Which epic.** The column axis is `EPIC-08`'s bar. This story is filed under
`EPIC-07` because these clips broke `EPIC-07`'s held-out score and its
absolute half. It must satisfy both epics' zero clips, and must not loosen
`EPIC-08`'s side-edge suites.

**Sizing.** The two kinds may have two different causes. Open question 2
decides whether this stays one story or is split before RED. The criteria

crop, which [MC-064](MC-064.md) pins as a known exception, and the story
empties that exception. "Both margins" means the two margins the corpus
suites test (0, the default, and 3).

  `2025-12-08 17_22_50.png` (now `tuning`), **when** it is processed at both
  margins, **then**:
  - the crop contains its mark on all four sides, so its right edge is at

  - its rows still lie within 167..1400 (no browser or taskbar row).
  - It is taken off every known-exception list.

  `2025-08-07 01_13_55.png`, **when** it is processed at both margins,
  **then**:
  - the crop contains its mark, with its right edge at column 1522 or beyond;
  - its rows lie within 115..1400.
  - It is taken off every known-exception list.
- **AC-3** — *`f18` does not crop the wrong region.* **Given**
  `2025-03-06 12_48_06.png`, **when** it is processed at both margins,
  **then**, per Open question 3's answer, one of these holds:
  - **(recommended)** the crop contains the mark, with columns covering

  - the crop contains its mark;

  - Every other marked `tuning` crop is exactly what `main` produces at both

  - Every known-exception list MC-064 added is **empty**.
  - `EPIC-08`'s side-edge suites (`corpus_sides.rs`, `corpus_page_column.rs`)

## Open questions — answer before it starts

Put to the user in these words.

1. **May I look at these four screenshots to work out what went wrong?**
   They are already practice screenshots once MC-064 is done, so looking
   costs nothing further. Nobody has looked at them yet.
   - *Recommended: yes*, the four only, at full size near the edges that
     failed. The other 21 new screenshots stay unexamined.
   - *Alternative:* no looking. The fix works from numbers alone, which is
     slower and may guess wrong.
2. **These look like two different problems. Fix them together or
   separately?** Two crops stop a few columns short on the right. The other
   two crop a different part of the screen, maybe a second window.
   - *Recommended: find the cause of each first, then split into two stories
     if the causes differ.* The right-edge one is probably small. The
     wrong-window one may need the same kind of work as the split-screen fix
     (MC-052).
   - *Alternative:* one story for all four.
3. **For the screenshot where the app cropped the wrong part of the screen:
   if the app cannot tell which window is the reader, is it acceptable to
   leave that screenshot alone (copy it unchanged, flagged) instead of
   cropping?**
   - *Recommended: the crop must get the right window.* Leaving it alone is
     allowed only as a stopgap, and only if finding the right window turns
     out not to be possible. Cutting the art is never acceptable.
   - *Alternative:* leaving it alone is an acceptable result.
4. **The fourth screenshot was not cut, but the crop was far too wide and
   kept the browser's scrollbar at the bottom.** Is it enough that the
   scrollbar goes? Or must the sides also come in to the art, which is the
   side-edges goal (`EPIC-08`)?
   - *Recommended: the scrollbar must go, and the art must be kept whole.*
     How tight the sides are is left to `EPIC-08`'s own checks.
   - *Alternative:* require the sides to hug the art here too.
