*Cause A, the close call.* The nearly-chrome strip is the Bottom strip

The page's own bottom rows sit below an art edge at row 1365: a white gap
(the mark's `gap: white`) between two dark margins. The close call is a real
one under MC-069's rule, because had the strip tipped over 0.85 it would have
been peeled and those rows clipped. The candidate fix is a statement of what
chrome is, as `viewport.rs` already reads it (painted edge to edge): **a Top or
Bottom strip whose tone over the page column differs from its own median by
