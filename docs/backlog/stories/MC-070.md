## Context

Split from MC-069 on the user's answer of 2026-10-01 ("Its own story").
`Screenshot (2705).png` (`toongod`, dark, 2560x1440) answers
`Flag(Ambiguous)` on `main`, for a different reason from MC-069's thirteen.
Measured by the Lead PO on `dabd249` (MC-069's `## Amendments`):

- After `content_box` peels Top, Bottom, Right, Bottom, the nearly-chrome
  strip is the **Bottom** strip `0,1366 2545x26`, flat fraction 0.848708,
  inside `[0.8475, 0.85)`. It lies inside the crop: the page's own bottom
  rows. MC-069's rule (a close call outside the crop does not flag) does not
  clear it.
- Even unflagged, the crop is `1073,133 399x1259` and the user's mark is
  `1073,133 400x1259`: column 1472 (mean luma 35.1, max 255) is art against
  background 11 at column 1473. A one-column clip at margin 0.

MC-069 adds the file to `tuning` with its mark and names it as the one
known `Ambiguous` entry (`KNOWN_AMBIGUOUS`, exact in both directions).

## Acceptance criteria

Drafted; re-cut by RED's measurement if needed (before RED, no Amendment).
