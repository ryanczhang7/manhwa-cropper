## Context

[MC-071](MC-071.md)'s held-out run (scored commit `4539e12`) cut one column
of `n05`, `2025-11-01 12_34_31.png` (`toongod`, dark, 2560x1440):

- crop at margin 0: columns **1007..1538**, rows 167..1400 (end exclusive);
- the user's mark: `1006,167 532x1233`, columns **1006..1538**;
- MC-071's frozen oracle: `T` 167, `B` 1400. The rows are exact; the only
  fault is the left column.

[MC-072](MC-072.md) moves it to `tuning` and names it in the zero-clip
suites' `KNOWN_CLIPS`, pinned to today's crop. This story removes it.

### Measured, and ruled by the user (2026-10-02, before RED)

The Lead PO measured column 1006 on `main` at `22194c1`, with a probe in a
scratch worktree outside the repository (the shipped `flat.rs` with an
`eprintln!` in `Margin::belongs`, and a per-column statistics example). The
numbers, over the mark's rows 167..1400 and over the central band 320..1160,
against a site background (`page_background_tone`) of **11**:

| column | mark rows: mean / median / min..max / share within 10 of 11 | band: median / min..max / share within 10 of own median |
|---|---|---|
| 1005 (site) | 11.0 / 11 / 11..11 / 1.000 | 11 / 11..11 / 1.000 |
| **1006** | 13.4 / 9 / 0..255 / 0.981 | **10** / 2..26 / 0.999 |
| 1007 (art) | 68.4 / 71 / 0..255 / 0.149 | 71 / 1..168 / 0.500 |

`Margin::belongs(1006)`: median 10, page background over the band, at the
tone (|10 - 11| <= 10) and not continuing the page edge (|10 - 71| > 10), so
**branch 1** returns margin. The widening stops at 1007; `locate_column`
gives `1007..1538`, and `detect` gives `1007,167 531x1233`.

**It reads like two columns the user's marks leave out**, on the same site
and layout:

| entry | column 1006, band median / range / share | mark starts at | crop starts at |
|---|---|---|---|
| `n05` `2025-11-01 12_34_31.png` | 10 / 2..26 / 0.999 | 1006 | 1007 |
| `Screenshot (3605).png` | 5 / 0..18 / 0.994 (tone 10) | **1007** | 1007 |
| `2025-10-20 15_37_25.png` | 13 / 2..26 / 0.988 | **1008** | 1007 |
| `f09` `2025-12-08 17_22_50.png` | 10 / 4..15 / 1.000 | 1006 | 1006 |

`f09` keeps its 1006 only because its column 1007 is dark art too (band
median 5, not page background), so 1006 continues the page's tone and
branch 3 admits it. On `n05`, (3605) and `10_20`, column 1006 is a thin dark
line beside bright art. MC-070's candidate B moved exactly (3605) and `10_20`
outward by that column, so a rule keeping `n05`'s would move them again. A
zoomed picture of the four edges was shown to the user.

Open question 1 was put to the user in plain words with those numbers. **The
user answered "Box starts at 1007"** (the recommended option): column 1006 is
the seam, not art, as on (3605) and `10_20`. So `n05`'s mark becomes
`1007,167 531x1233`, which today's crop already equals at margin 0, and this
story is the re-mark and taking `n05` off every list MC-072 put it on. No
production code changes. The type is now `chore` (no Amendment: the story is
PLANNED).
