## Context

**The request (2026-09-30).** After MC-059's negative, the user chose a fresh
set: *"we can get a fresh set. instead of me sourcing them, can you search in
the folders I have specified before for screenshots not already cropped, that
havent been used before."* Their answers to the follow-up questions, the same
day:

- **Folders:** the eight the corpus came from and nothing else ("everything is
  already in. dont include anything else"): `Downloads\mahwa panels`, and in
  `OneDrive\Pictures\Screenshots 1` the series folders Demonic Evolution,
  Eleceed, Hero Killer, Jungle Juice, Pick Me Up Infinite Gacha, Unholy Blood
  and Suicidal Battle God.
- **"Not already cropped"** means whole-screen captures only, not the user's
  own trimmed or stitched panel images.
- **Picked** at random, spread over the series, by an agent that does not look
  at them.
- **Space:** raise the corpus ceiling, `MAX_BYTES`, from 60 MiB to 100 MiB.
  The corpus is 61,587,028 bytes (58.7 MiB) today.

**Why a representative set, and not a hunt for site headers.** The user
pointed out that a site's own header shows only when the page is scrolled to
the top of a chapter. So a held-out set chosen to contain headers would test
something rarer than real use. A random draw of whole-screen captures shows
headers about as often as real use does.

  exactly one entry per drawn file, each with:

    answered "can't tell");

  tuning's, and was watched to fail before the entries existed.

  watched to fail at 60 MiB with the 25 files in place.

  before this story (MC-051 spent 26; MC-056 already moved 3) have `split: "tuning"`, and

  every marked tuning entry, flags flagged, crops pinned where a suite pins
