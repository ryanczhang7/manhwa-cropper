## Context

[MC-078](MC-078.md) took `EPIC-07`'s fourth held-out score, on MC-077's 10
fresh screenshots, at scored commit `c108bd1`: **1 clip of 10, and 9 of 10
meeting the bar against 9** ([`held-out-score.md`](../../wiki/held-out-score.md),
"MC-078, 2026-10-04"). Only the failing entry was read per file (MC-078
`## Notes`, the AC-3 output, verbatim):

| MC-077 id | File | Reader | Why it failed (margin 0, end exclusive) | Mark (unchanged by this story) | `T` / `B` (MC-078's frozen oracle) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `n05` | `2025-10-26 12_13_16.png` | `toongod` | **clip of one column**: columns 974..1573 against the mark's 973..1573; rows 167..1400, exact; no furniture | `973,167 600x1233` | 167 / 1400 |

Having been read per file, it is `tuning` for any later rule (`corpus.md`,
"Spent by MC-078"). Its fix, [MC-080](MC-080.md), is designed while looking
at it, and a held-out entry a rule is designed against cannot stay
`held-out`. The precedents are [MC-064](MC-064.md) and [MC-072](MC-072.md).
MC-078 was a spike and could not write `fixtures/`, so the move is this
chore.

**What the move is.** One `"split"` value in
`fixtures/corpus/manifest.json` changes from `held-out` to `tuning`. No
`expect`, no tag and no byte changes: **the corpus adds no file**. The entry
then enters every tuning suite, where it clips by one column at margin 0, so
it is named as a **known clip, exact in both directions**, until MC-080
removes it.

**After the move** (counted from the manifest at `79a6576`): held-out holds
**9 marked and 0 flag** entries across **4 readers** (`toongod` 4,
`rolia-scans` 2, `xbato` 2, `w-network` 1); tuning holds **100 marked and
14 flag**.

**The floors (the user's ruling, 2026-10-04).** Asked in plain words, with
the note that the 9 left are spent and the next fresh draw sets the floors
again, the user answered **"9 boxes, 4 sites"**: `MIN_HELD_OUT_MARKED` 10 ->
**9**; `MIN_HELD_OUT_SITES` stays **4**; `MIN_HELD_OUT_FLAGS` 0 and
`MIN_UNSEEN_SITES` 0 stand.

**The route: RED, then SCAFFOLD**, as MC-072's. `fixtures/corpus/manifest.json`
and every file under `crates/engine/tests/` classify as `test`. RED writes
the pins and the exception lists and watches them fail against the unmoved
manifest; SCAFFOLD flips the one split and writes `corpus.md`. No production
source is written.

**Required gate that would fail if the artifact broke:** `unit`
(`corpus_manifest.rs`) for AC-1 and AC-2; `integration` for AC-3 and AC-4,
hence `required_gates: [integration]`.

here is MC-077's `n05`, `2025-10-26 12_13_16.png`.

  - `READER_BY_FILE`, `READER_LABELS` and `MARKED_TUNING_RECTS` name the

    fails it (probe recorded).

    with a doc-comment paragraph naming this story and the user's ruling in
    their words. *Control:* with a tenth held-out entry missing, or with any
    one reader gone from held-out, the floor test fails (shown in RED by
    dropping entries from the test's view, not by editing the manifest).
- **AC-3** — *The known clip, exact in both directions.*
  - `n05` is named in every zero-clip test that fails on it (the
    `KNOWN_CLIPS` lists in `corpus.rs`, `corpus_accuracy.rs`,

    not a ruling that the column is or is not art.

    fails if a listed entry's crop differs from its pin.
