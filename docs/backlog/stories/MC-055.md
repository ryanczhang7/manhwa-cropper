## Context

**This is MC-053 with a different success condition.** MC-053 was the fix for
the page column locator trimming the outer columns of dark, low-texture art.
Read its `## Context`, `## Notes` and `## Amendments` first. On 2026-09-27 it
became a spike: GREEN's rule passed every test, but one of its branches was
fitted to one file, `2025-07-17 14_41_58.png`, by a 3-grey-level margin.
The user chose investigation only.

**The spike's verdict** (MC-053 `## Notes`, "Spike verdict"):

- GREEN's branches 1 and 2 are general, and this story may take them as its
  design:
  - stop at a column that is at the margin's tone and not at the tone of the
    column inside it;
  - otherwise, extend through columns that are not page background over the
    central band.
- Together they fix `14_55_10` and `(73)`. They hold the 23 originals exactly
  and let no page background in. `14_41_58` still clips 7 columns on the left
  and 4 on the right at margin 0.
- `14_41_58`'s art columns look like page background over the central band:
  0.95–0.99 share, and a median 3 levels from the page tone. They read as art
  only over the viewport's rows. The leads, both unmeasured: texture over the
  viewport's rows, with an argument for those rows; or continuity with the art
  column just inside.

**Where to start.** Branch `wip/MC-053-dark-art-candidate` (`e194750`)
holds all of this:
- MC-053's RED tests, which fail for the right reason on `c004d96`;
- the data edits the user ruled on (`docs/wiki/corpus.md`, "MC-053's
  rulings, pending MC-055");
- the seven re-pointed tests, approved by the Lead PO on 2026-09-25;
- GREEN's rule.

RED here merges `main` into that work, re-measures on the result, and keeps
MC-053's handoff tables as the baseline. GREEN starts from branches 1 and 2,
**not** from branch 3.

**The held-out hold.** Until this story lands, no held-out score is taken
(`corpus.md`). This story's GATES runs the held-out aggregate once, as
MC-053's AC-5 says. The baseline is 19 entries, 18 cropped, 1 `Ambiguous`,
0 clips at both margins.

### GREEN: the rule (Feature Developer, 2026-09-28)

**In plain words.** As on MC-053, the widest textured run over the central
band *finds* the page. The page column is that run widened outward one column
at a time, and the widening stops at the first column that is margin. A
column is judged by these three branches, in order:

1. **Margin: the margin's tone and not the page's.** Its band median is
   within `uniform_tolerance` of the page background tone and *not* within
   `uniform_tolerance` of the column just inside it. This is MC-053's
   branch 1, unchanged.
2. **Page: not page background over the band.** MC-049's predicate, whatever
   the column's spread. This is MC-053's branch 2, unchanged.
3. **Page: flat over the band, but the page carried on.** New, and it
   replaces MC-053's branch 3. A column that *is* page background over the
   band is still page when both of these hold:
   - its band median is within `uniform_tolerance` of the column just inside
     it (continuity, lead (b));
   - it is **not** page background over the viewport's rows (lead (a)).

   Otherwise it is margin, and so is any such column when no viewport is
   located.

After the widening, MC-027's interior rule is applied once, as before.

**The argument, which is also in `flat.rs`'s "Stage 3c" comment.**
- MC-049's predicate is defined over the *page's* rows. The band stands in
  for them only because it is safe from the chrome, and a dark page can be
  flat all the way through it.
- The viewport is the measured set of rows between the chrome and the
  taskbar, so it holds every row the page can be on. The margin beside the
  page is flat on those rows by the viewport's own definition.
- A flat column that is art continues the page's tone. Requiring that keeps
  the viewport reading from speaking for a column whose tone has already
  handed over.
- The tone is not read in branch 3 at all. MC-053's `median != tone` guard,
  the exact comparison the spike found fitted, is gone.
