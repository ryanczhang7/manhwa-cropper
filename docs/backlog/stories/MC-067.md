## Context

**Filed 2026-10-01** by the user's answer *"Ship f20, file f09 apart"*
during [MC-065](MC-065.md)'s GREEN (its `## Amendments`).

`f09`, `2025-12-08 17_22_50.png` (`toongod`, `tuning`): the app's crop is
`1006,167 531x1233` at margin 0, and the mark, which the user ruled stands
(2026-09-30), is `1006,167 533x1233`. The crop cuts **2 columns of art on the
right** (1537..1538), the art's near-black outer edge. The user ruled those
columns art from their colour: dark reds and greys, no blue, against the
site's navy `2,13,24` (MC-065 `## Notes`).

**Why it could not ship in MC-065.** In luma (BT.601), `f09`'s right fringe
is indistinguishable from `Screenshot (3538).png`'s two fringes (same reader,
same site value 11). Each page edge ends in one column near the site on about
half its rows, then one on about 90%. MC-065's `## Notes` (2026-10-01) has the
Lead PO's measurement. Any luma rule that keeps `f09`'s fringe widens 3538's
crop by 2 columns per side, onto the near-flat grey columns the user objected
to in MC-053 (its Open question 5). MC-053's frozen pins and MC-048's
`COLUMNS_BEFORE` hold 3538 exactly.

**The mechanism** (MC-065 `## Handoff`): stage 3c's widening,
`Margin::belongs` branch 1 in `crates/core/src/flat.rs`, calls column 1537
margin because its band median (15) is within `uniform_tolerance` of the
site's tone (11). It never checks that the column is page background
(share 0.882).
