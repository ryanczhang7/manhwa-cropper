## Context

[MC-071](MC-071.md)'s held-out run (scored commit `4539e12`) cut `n02`,
`2025-03-07 00_05_58.png` (`toongod`, dark, 2560x1440), on **both** sides:

- crop at margin 0: columns **703..1101**, rows **0..1440** (end exclusive);
- the user's mark: `643,115 533x1284`, columns **643..1176**, rows 115..1399;
- so 60 columns of art are cut on the left and 75 on the right, and the crop
  also runs the full screen height: browser chrome, the horizontal scrollbar
  (ruled at row 1399) and the taskbar (1400);
- MC-071's frozen oracle: `T` 115, `B` 1399.

[MC-072](MC-072.md) moves it to `tuning` and pins today's crop in the
zero-clip suites' `KNOWN_CLIPS`. This story removes it from them.

**What this story owns: the columns.** `n02`'s rows are
[MC-075](MC-075.md)'s, unless the measurement shows they share this cause
(below).

**Nothing below is a finding.** No agent has measured this image. Candidate
causes, from MC-071's numbers and the corpus pins:

- **Candidate A: the page-column locator settles on a band inside the
  art.** 703..1101 is wholly inside 643..1176. The outer 60 and 75 columns
  may be dark or low-texture art read as margin, the territory of
  [MC-053](MC-053.md) and [MC-055](MC-055.md) ("the page column keeps dark,
  low-texture art").
- **Candidate B: the columns and the rows are one fault.** The viewport stage
  is composed beside the pipeline's page column (`corpus_viewport_stage.rs`),
  and MC-064 measured that `f18` declined because its page column was wrong
  (`1828,0 717x1440`, full height). A wrong page column here may make the
  stage decline, which would explain the full-height rows; or a declined
  stage may leave the locator reading chrome and taskbar rows, which would
  explain the columns. Which way the arrow runs is the measurement.
- **Evidence that the layout alone is not the cause:** two `tuning` entries
  from the same reader and the same evening, `2025-03-07 00_41_10.png` and
  `2025-03-07 01_10_37.png`, have their viewport located at 115..1399
  (`STAGE_MEASURED`) and crop without a clip. `n10` (held-out, never read per
  file, and not read here) has the same mark columns 643..1176 and is not
  among MC-071's failures.

MC-072's RED records whether the stage declines on `n02`.

**Before RED, the Lead PO measures:** the pipeline's page column for `n02`
and `viewport::locate` beside it; the column profile across 600..1220 over
the mark's rows (what the locator sees at 643..703 and 1101..1176); and the
same with the rows restricted to 115..1399. If restricting the rows alone
recovers 643..1176, candidate B holds, and this story and MC-075's `n02`
half are **merged** into one (re-cut before RED, the user told). Otherwise
they stay separate.

## Acceptance criteria

Drafted; re-cut by the Lead PO's measurement before RED (see `## Context`).

- **AC-1** — *`n02`'s crop spans its page.* **Given**
  `2025-03-07 00_05_58.png`, **when** it is processed at both margins,
  **then** the outcome is `Cropped` and the crop contains the mark's columns
  643..1176 (left edge at 643 or left of it, right edge at 1176 or beyond),
  and at margin 0 keeps no column of page background outside the mark
  (`EPIC-08`'s side predicate, unloosened). It is taken off every MC-072 list
  that names it for its columns (`KNOWN_CLIPS`, `KNOWN_MISSES`, the crop
  pins, `KNOWN_BACKGROUND_SIDES` if listed), and its `MAIN_CROPS` rows are
  re-measured. Red on `main` (after MC-072): today's crop is 703..1101.
  If the measurement merges this story with MC-075's `n02` half, AC-1 also
  requires rows within 115..1399 (`T`, `B`) and `n02` leaves
  `STAGE_DECLINED` (if it is there) with a measured `STAGE_MEASURED` row.
- **AC-2** — *Nothing else moves, and no clip anywhere.*
  - Every other marked `tuning` crop is exactly what `main` produces at both
    margins (`corpus_tuning_crops_unmoved.rs`); any re-measured row is named
    and explained in `## Notes`.
  - Zero clips holds on every marked `tuning` entry at both margins, except
    the entries MC-073, MC-075 and MC-076 own while they remain listed.
  - Every flag entry stays flagged. MC-052's two split-screen entries and
    MC-066's second-window entries keep their columns.
  - `EPIC-08`'s side-edge suites pass unloosened.
  - **Controls:** the one-column-wider control still fires; reverting the
    fix turns AC-1 red on `n02` (probe recorded in `## Notes`).
- **AC-3** — *A generated reproduction*, in the required `unit` gate, of the
  cause the Lead PO measured: a generated page with `n02`'s shape (for
  candidate A, art whose outer bands are the low-texture kind the locator
  drops) whose page column comes out narrower than the art on `main`, and a
  **control** on the same geometry differing only in the cause (for
  candidate A, the outer bands given the inner art's texture) whose page
  column is the whole art today and after. If it cannot be built to fail
  today, `## Notes` says why, with the attempt's output, and AC-1 carries the
  regression alone.
