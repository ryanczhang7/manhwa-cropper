## Context

Composes the pipeline from `architecture.md` - trim (MC-003), content box
(MC-005), a second trim inside the box, then the outward margin - into
`detect(&Luma, &Tuning) -> Option<Detection>`, and establishes the brief's
feature 2, "never clip artwork", as a property checked with `proptest` over
random recipes rather than a handful of examples. `Detection` carries the
final rect and what the stages did (`trimmed: bool`, `removed: Vec<Side>`,
`ambiguous: bool`) so MC-007 can turn it into a decision. `margin_px = 3`.

Gate that fails if this breaks: `unit`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** - Given a fixture with uniform borders on all sides and a chrome band at the top, when `detect` runs, then `rect` equals the art rect expanded by `margin_px` on every side and clamped to the image.
- **AC-2** - Given a fixture where full-width chrome sits above a page that has uniform gutters left and right of the art (so the first trim removes nothing because the top rows are chrome), when `detect` runs, then the chrome is peeled, the gutters are trimmed by the second trim, and `rect` is the art rect plus margin.
- **AC-3** - Given the art rect touches the image's right edge, when `detect` runs, then `rect.x + rect.w == width` (the margin clamps rather than overflows), and the other three sides carry the full margin.
- **AC-4** - *Property, proptest, at least 256 cases:* given any recipe the generator can produce (per-side border widths 0..=200 and colours 0..=255, either layout, zero to two chrome bands per edge with flat fraction in 0.86..=0.98 and heights up to 25% of the dimension, art size 100..=1200 per side, seeded texture), when `detect` returns `Some(d)`, then the art rect is contained in `d.rect` (never clip) and `d.rect` is contained in the art rect expanded by `margin_px + 2` on every side (never much junk). *Control on the metric:* the orchestrator mutates `margin` to shrink by 1 px on the top instead of expanding and records in `## Notes` that the property fails.
- **AC-5** - Given a uniform image, when `detect` runs, then it returns `None`.

## Model guidance

Model: default. Oracle partition:

- **Settled**: every constant in `Tuning::default()`, including `margin_px = 3`. The `+2` slack in AC-4 is settled too.
- **Mechanical**: `detect(&Luma, &Tuning) -> Option<Detection>`, `Detection { rect: Rect, trimmed: bool, removed: Vec<Side>, ambiguous: bool }`. Pin.
- **Oracle-free**: none - the generator knows the art rect. If the property finds a recipe that fails, that is a real finding about the algorithm, not the test; shrink it, record it in the handoff, and do not narrow the generator to avoid it without an `## Amendments` entry approved by the user.

## Out of scope

Flag reasons and the `CropDecision` type (MC-007), anything below 1 px of
precision, a margin that varies with image size (decision 5), performance
(MC-017).
