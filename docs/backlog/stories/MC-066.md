## Acceptance criteria

Each criterion reproduces the bug first. Its test is red against today's
crop, which [MC-064](MC-064.md) pins as a known exception, and the story
removes that exception. "Both margins" means the two margins the corpus
suites test: 0 (the default) and 3. Column bounds are end-exclusive. The
seam columns were measured by the Lead PO from the pixels on 2026-09-30, and
RED re-measures them.

- **AC-1** — *`f18` is cropped to the reader's window.* **Given**
  `2025-03-06 12_48_06.png`, **when** it is processed at both margins,
  **then** the outcome is `Cropped` and the crop:
  - contains the mark: columns cover 651..1168, rows cover 115..1399;
  - lies within rows 115..1399 (no tab strip, bookmarks bar or taskbar row);
  - ends at column 1828 or left of it, so **no column of the second window**
    is kept.

  A `Flagged` outcome does **not** satisfy this criterion. By the user's
  answer to Open question 3 ("Must crop right"), leaving it alone is allowed
  only as a stopgap, and only if finding the right window turns out not to
  be possible. That needs an `## Amendments` entry the user approves, with
  the evidence that it is not possible. It is never pre-authorised here.
  Cutting the art is never acceptable.
- **AC-2** — *`f13` stays in the reader's window and drops the scrollbar.*
  **Given** `2025-03-16 22_47_44.png`, **when** it is processed at both
  margins, **then** the crop:
  - contains the mark: columns cover 635..1168, rows cover 115..1392;
  - has its bottom edge at row 1392 or above, so no scrollbar row is kept;
  - ends at column 1820 or left of it, so no column of the second window is
    kept.

  How tightly the right side hugs the art **inside** the reader's window is
  left to `EPIC-08`'s own checks, by the user's answer to Open question 4
  ("Stay in the reader window").
- **AC-3** — *Nothing else moves, and no clip anywhere.*
  - `f13` and `f18` are taken off every MC-064 list they are on:
    - `KNOWN_CLIPS`, `f18`, in `corpus.rs`, `corpus_accuracy.rs`,
      `corpus_sides.rs` and `corpus_viewport.rs`;
    - `KNOWN_MISSES`, both;
    - `MC064_CROPS`, both;
    - `KNOWN_BACKGROUND_SIDES`, `f13`, in `corpus_sides.rs` and
      `corpus_page_column.rs`;
    - `STAGE_DECLINED`, `f18`, in `corpus_viewport.rs` and
      `corpus_viewport_stage.rs`.

    `STAGE_MEASURED` gains a measured row for `f18`, and `f13`'s row is
    re-measured. Both have their `MAIN_CROPS` rows re-measured. MC-065's
    entries keep their pins.
  - Every other marked `tuning` crop is exactly what `main` produces at both
    margins (`corpus_tuning_crops_unmoved.rs`). The only exception is a row
    re-measured and listed, named and explained in `## Notes`. MC-052's two
    split-screen entries are named in that check and must not move.

    the entries MC-065 owns while they remain listed.
  - `EPIC-08`'s side-edge suites (`corpus_sides.rs`,
    `corpus_page_column.rs`) pass unloosened.
