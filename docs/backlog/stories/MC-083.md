## Context

[MC-081](MC-081.md) records the user's report of 2026-10-04 and adds seven of
their Eleceed screenshots to `tuning`. On one, **e03,
`2025-03-07 01_02_31.png`**, the app cropped the manhwa page in the reader's
window on the left, but kept the **whole screen height**: the bookmarks bar
at the top and the taskbar at the bottom. The other six took the YouTube
window on the right instead ([MC-082](MC-082.md)).

**Why it is its own story, for now.** Its symptom is the rows, not the
window. It resembles [MC-075](MC-075.md)'s `n06`
(`2025-03-13 12_01_01.png`), also a split screen kept at full height: there
the viewport stage declined, because the second window has no flat viewport
of its own and the whole-margin share never reached `PAGE_LIKE`. MC-075
fixed that and `n06` now passes. MC-052's `2025-03-07 00_58_06.png`, about 4
minutes before e03 in the same session, passes too. So whatever makes e03
fail is not the split screen alone. **Nothing here is a finding**; no agent
has measured e03. Candidates:
- the viewport stage declines on e03, by a condition MC-075's reader-window
  reading does not reach (for instance the reader's window's own flat share,
  or a YouTube page rather than a video on the right);
- the page column is right only by luck, and its wrong viewport is a side
  effect of the same cause as MC-082's six.

**Before RED, the Lead PO measures** (once MC-081 is DONE, on `main`, with a
probe in a scratch worktree outside the repository): `viewport::locate`
beside the pipeline's page column for e03; if it declines, which of the
stage's own conditions refuses, with its row profile at the bookmarks bar's
end and the taskbar's start; and the same read-out beside `n06` and
`00_58_06`. The furniture rows `T` (end of the browser bar) and `B` (start of
the scrollbar or the taskbar) are measured and put to the user with a zoomed
picture, then frozen, as MC-071's were. **If the measurement shows the same
cause as MC-082's six, the two stories merge before RED** and the user is
told. The criteria below are a draft, re-cut after measurement under
`## Amendments`.

**No suite may judge these rows today.** MC-075 found that a declined
viewport stage leaves `corpus_viewport.rs` nothing to judge, and added
`corpus_furniture_rows.rs`, which checks crops against frozen `T` and `B`
for named entries (`MC071_FURNITURE`). MC-081's RED records whether any
`tuning` test fails on e03. If none does, this story adds e03 to that check,
and that is what turns it red.

**Required gate that would fail if the artifact broke:** `integration` for
AC-1 and AC-2, hence `required_gates: [integration]`. AC-3 is in the
required `unit` gate.

## Acceptance criteria

**Draft, to be re-cut after the measurement in `## Context`.** "Both
margins" means 0 and 3. Bounds are end-exclusive. `T` and `B` are e03's
furniture rows, measured before RED and frozen by the user.

- **AC-1** — *e03 drops the furniture.* **Given**
  `2025-03-07 01_02_31.png`, **when** it is processed at both margins,
  **then** the outcome is `Cropped` and the crop:
  - contains the user's mark (MC-081's frozen table);
  - lies within rows `T..B`: no tab strip, bookmarks bar, scrollbar or
    taskbar row;
  - keeps no column of the second window (it ends at or left of the seam,
    measured as MC-082 measures it).

  The test is red against today's crop: e03 is added to
  `corpus_furniture_rows.rs`'s frozen rows (or the suite the measurement
  shows to be the right one) with its `T` and `B`, and fails on `main`
  naming e03.
- **AC-2** — *Nothing else moves, and no clip anywhere.*
  - e03 is taken off every list MC-081 put it on.
  - Every other marked `tuning` crop is exactly what `main` produces at both
    margins (`corpus_tuning_crops_unmoved.rs`), e03's row re-measured. The
    split-screen entries `n06`, `n02` `2025-03-07 00_05_58.png`,
    `2025-03-07 00_58_06.png`, `2025-03-06 01_22_45.png`, `f13` and `f18` are
    named in that check and must not move; every `STAGE_MEASURED` row but
    e03's is unchanged.
  - `MC071_FURNITURE`'s four keep passing, and its `KNOWN_FURNITURE_KEPT`
    stays empty.
  - Zero clips holds on every marked `tuning` entry at both margins, except
    MC-082's six while they remain listed.
  - **Control:** reverting the fix turns AC-1 red on e03 (probe in
    `## Notes`).
- **AC-3** — *A generated reproduction of the measured cause,* in the
  required `unit` gate, red against today's code, with a control that
  switches the cause off and leaves everything else the same (MC-075's
  `n06`-shaped scene is the precedent). If it cannot be built to fail today,
  `## Notes` says why, with the attempt's output.

## Model guidance

Model: default. The Lead PO measures before RED and orchestrates; the Test
Developer writes RED; the Feature Developer writes GREEN.

**Oracle partition.**
- **Settled, read out:** e03's mark (MC-081); `T` and `B` once frozen; zero
  clips; today's crop (MC-081's pin).
- **Oracle-free: the cause.** Stated with a control that turns it on and off
  (AC-3), not read off one image.
- **Mechanical, pin exactly:** AC-1's row and column bounds, AC-2's list
  removals and unmoved crops.
- **A person's call:** Open question 1.

**Success condition, which can come out either way:** after GREEN, e03 is
`Cropped` within `T..B`, contains its mark and keeps no column of the second
window; no other crop or stage row moves unlisted; the integration command
with `--no-fail-fast` is green. It **fails** on a clip anywhere outside
MC-082's listed entries, any named split-screen entry moving, a threshold
swept until e03 passes without a cause stated and controlled first, or the
furniture check loosened for any of MC-071's four.
