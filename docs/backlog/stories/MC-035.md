## Context

MC-034 measured the row axis with two families over 780 parameterisations
(`docs/wiki/panel-edge-search.md`) and scored every entry against an **exact**
mark. On 2026-09-17, after MC-034 was DONE, the user ruled that three entries
carry a **band**: on `Screenshot (2708).jpg`, `Screenshot (3538).png` and
`Screenshot (2698).jpg`, a rect ending at the **top of the gutter** and one
ending at the **recorded mark** are equally right. The ruling is on
`docs/wiki/corpus.md` under "The gutter-crossing tolerance"; EPIC-05's closing
amendment and MC-032's parked section item 1 both point here.

So MC-034's bottom-edge numbers are a **lower bound**: some entries it scored as
misses or clips were producing an acceptable answer.

**How much of that lower bound is slack turns out to be mostly derivable, and
one part of it is not.** The derivable part is below and is read out, not
measured. The part that is not is the **clip count** — and it matters more than
the rest, because MC-005 decision 13 ranks a clip above every other defect and
MC-034's best clip-free rule stands at **0 of 21**, the worst number in its
report. This story asks one question: **does admitting the band retire any
clips, and does it lift the clip-free best off zero?**

### The widened predicate is a disjunction, not a wider window

This is the single most likely way to get the measurement wrong, so it is stated
before the criteria rather than discovered afterwards. MC-019 scores a bottom
edge `b` against an expected bottom `e` as: `b >= e` (no clip) **and**
`b <= e + 11` (inside the margin-expanded window). The band gives two expected
bottoms, so the widened predicate is a **disjunction of two exact MC-019
scorings** — the entry is a hit if it is a hit against *either* — and a clip only
if it clips *both*. It is **not** `e_gutter <= b <= e_mark + 11`, which is a
different and more generous thing.

The distinction is not academic. Worked out from the table above:

| Entry | acceptable bottoms under the disjunction | what the naive widening would also admit |
|---|---|---|
| `Screenshot (2708).jpg` | `[1305,1316] ∪ [1322,1333]` | rows 1317–1321 |
| `Screenshot (3538).png` | `[1314,1325] ∪ [1330,1341]` | rows 1326–1329 |
| `Screenshot (2698).jpg` | `[1080,1096]` (contiguous; the 5 px band is inside the 11 px window) | — |

A rect ending at row 1318 on `2708` is 13 px below the gutter top and 4 px above
the mark: it is a hit against neither of the two answers the user ruled
acceptable, and admitting it is the oracle being edited rather than read. The
arithmetic above is derived from frozen inputs and the document is expected to
restate it in its own terms; if it comes out differently, the difference is the
finding and it goes to the user before anything is scored.

### Why this story is only about clips: the rest was settled by derivation, not left to measure

**This story was deliberately narrowed by the product owner on 2026-09-17,
after the derivation below was worked out while drafting it and checked against
MC-034's committed table.** The first draft asked "how much of the row-axis
deficit does the tolerance recover" and required a full before-and-after on the
ceiling, the per-edge reachability and the headline. Most of that is derivable
in advance and measuring it would have been waste. What follows is therefore a
**settled input read out here**, not a result this story produces:

This story is **not** a rescue, and the brief that asked for it should not be
read as one. MC-034 section 2c's per-file table reads:

```
Screenshot (2698).jpg             yes      yes      yes
Screenshot (2708).jpg             yes      yes      yes
Screenshot (3538).png             yes      yes      yes
```

**All three band entries are already `both: yes`** — some parameterisation of
some family already places both their edges, without clipping, under the
**exact** mark. Widening a scoring predicate can only turn a `NO` into a `yes`,
never the reverse, and none of the eleven entries nothing places (`23_30_20`,
`15_37_25`, `13_33_41`, `13_45_59`, `13_49_39`, `70`, `75`, `93`, `103`, `2744`,
`3187`) carries a band. Therefore:

1. **The per-file ceiling stays at 10 of 21.** Bound on the movement: **zero**.
2. **The per-edge reachability stays at top 20 of 21, bottom 11 of 21.** Bound:
   **zero** on both.
3. The per-file ceiling dominates any single rule scored under the same
   predicate, so **the best single rule is still bounded above by 10 of 21**. It
   stands at 8 of 21, so the headline can move by at most **+2 entries**, not
   +3 — which is the brief's own arithmetic corrected against the table.

What *is* genuinely unmeasured, and is the deliverable:

- **the clip count.** MC-034 section 3c names all three entries as failures of
  the best coverage member in the "stops above the mark" shape — `2708` stops
  1305 against a mark of 1322, `3538` stops 1314 against 1330, `2698` stops 1080
  against 1085 — and under the exact mark a rect that stops above the mark is a
  **clip**, which MC-005 decision 13 ranks above every other defect. Up to three
  clips can be retired from a given parameterisation's count;
- **the best clip-free rule**, which stands at 0 of 21 and is the number MC-034
  section 5 says the coverage family loses on;
- **which parameterisation ranks best**, which can change, and with it
  AC-4(c)'s flag-crop exception.

So the expected shape of the answer is "the clip count falls, the clip-free best
may come off zero, and the parking decision stands". **That is a valid and
expected result**, in the same way MC-028's, MC-031's and MC-034's reasoned
negatives were.

**The narrowing, stated plainly so nobody re-widens it by accident.** Points 1
to 3 above are read out of MC-034 section 2c and are *not* re-measured here. The
headline rows number comes out of the same sweep as the clips and is reported
because it is free, not because it is the question. **The question is the clip
count and the clip-free best**, and the reason it is worth a story when the rest
is not: MC-005 decision 13 ranks a clip above every other defect, MC-034's best
clip-free rule is **0 of 21** — its single worst number — and all three band
entries are named in `panel-edge-search.md` section 3c as clips of the
"stops above the mark" shape. Whether retiring them lifts clip-free off zero is
**not** derivable from any table, and a clip-free rule is the only kind this
product could ever ship (`corpus.md`: a clip is the defect the whole design is
arranged against).

If the answer is that clip-free stays at 0 of 21, the row axis is closed on
measurement rather than on argument, which is what EPIC-05 recorded as
outstanding.

## Model guidance

Model: default. The verdict recorded in MC-034 `## Notes` says the default was
enough on a harder question under a partitioned brief, and this story is
strictly smaller: one predicate, one harness, no rule design. Oracle partition:

- **Settled — read out, never re-derived.** The three entries and their two
  acceptable bottoms (`corpus.md`, "The gutter-crossing tolerance"); the fact
  that the ruling covers three entries and a fourth is a person's call; MC-019's
  11 px window, its zero-clip rule and the manifest, fixed by the user's
  decisions of 2026-09-16 and 2026-09-17; MC-005 decision 13; every MC-034
  number this story compares against, including section 2c's per-file table and
  section 7's gutter ranges. These came from a person looking at images or from
  a completed measurement. An agent that "checks" them against a gradient will
  get a different answer and the different answer will be wrong — `corpus.md`
  says the diagonal instrument already did exactly that, and MC-034 section 2d
  says the flatness instrument did it a second time.
- **Oracle-free, and the whole of the work.** How the widened predicate is
  expressed in the harness; the three controls in AC-4 and the probes that show
  them firing. Control (c) is the one that has to be invented rather than
  copied: no prior spike needed a control on *the oracle's own width*, because
  no prior spike widened one. It must be shown firing, and a frozen band that a
  wider band cannot beat is a finding, not a nuisance.
- **Mechanical.** AC-2's reproduction, AC-3's reporting shape, AC-6.

**Success condition, which can come out either way.** This spike succeeds if a
reader can say, with numbers and commands: *before the band the best member
carried K clips and the best clip-free rule stood at 0 of 21; after it, K′ clips
and a clip-free best of Z of 21; the two controls fired; and here is what that
implies for whether a clip-free row rule could ever exist.* It fails if it
reports K′ without K, if either control passes vacuously, or if it reports a
ceiling or a per-edge reachability number at all — the last because `## Context`
derives that neither can move, and measuring them is the waste this story was
narrowed to avoid.

**Model-choice success condition, which can also come out either way.** The
condition that would overturn the default: if the investigation extends the band
to a fourth entry, expresses the predicate as one widened window rather than a
disjunction, reports a delta without AC-2's reproduction of MC-034's before
numbers, or reports the recovery without noticing that AC-1's bound caps it at
+2 — then the failure is one of judgement under a partitioned brief and a
stronger model is worth trying on the next spike of this shape. If the bound is
derived before the measurement and the controls fire, the default was enough.
**Record the verdict in `## Notes` when the investigation ends** — a model choice
with no recorded verdict is folklore.

**Timebox.** This is the narrowed story: the ceiling cannot move, the headline
is capped at +2 against a bar ten entries away, and the only open number is the
clip count. If AC-2's reproduction is clean and the two controls fire, the
measurement itself is one run of `sweep` and `sweep2` with a flag, plus `detail`
on the best member before and after. That is the whole of it — if the work is
growing past that, it is re-widening, and `## Context` says why it was narrowed.
If the harness does not build, or the reproduction does
not come back exactly, stop and report that rather than rebuilding the harness
— a harness that has drifted is a finding about MC-034's reproducibility and
belongs to the user, not to a rebuild.
