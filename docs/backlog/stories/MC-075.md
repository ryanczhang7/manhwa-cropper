## Context

[MC-071](MC-071.md)'s held-out run (scored commit `4539e12`) kept the whole
screen height on two `toongod` screenshots, both with a browser bar ending at
row 115 and a horizontal scrollbar the user ruled browser furniture:

| MC-071 id | File | Crop at margin 0 (end exclusive) | Mark | `T` / `B` (frozen oracle) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `n06` | `2025-03-13 12_01_01.png` | columns 698..1098 (exact), rows **0..1440** | `698,115 400x1277` | 115 / 1392 (scrollbar 1392, taskbar 1400) |
| `n02` | `2025-03-07 00_05_58.png` | columns 703..1101 (a clip, MC-074's), rows **0..1440** | `643,115 533x1284` | 115 / 1399 (scrollbar 1399, taskbar 1400) |

Each crop keeps the tab strip and toolbar above `T`, and the scrollbar and
taskbar below `B`. MC-071 records that the viewport stage declined on 3 of
15 and does **not** say which three. [MC-072](MC-072.md)'s RED records
whether it declines on these two.

**No suite judges these rows today if the stage declines.**
`corpus_viewport.rs`'s furniture predicate reads the rows the stage located;
a declined entry has none (MC-064's `f18`, `STAGE_DECLINED`). So this story
adds a corpus check against MC-071's frozen `T` and `B`, which are settled
oracle data, not the stage's output.

**Nothing below is a finding.** No agent has measured these images.
Candidates:

- **Candidate A: the stage declines on these two**, and with it declined no
  later stage removes the browser bar or the taskbar.
- **Candidate B (for `n02` only): its wrong page column makes the stage
  decline**, as `f18`'s did in MC-064. Then MC-074 fixes `n02`'s rows too,
  and this story keeps `n06` alone.
- **Evidence that the bar layout alone is not the cause:** `n10` (held-out,
  never read per file, and not read here) has exactly `n06`'s ruled bars
  (`T` 115, scrollbar 1392, taskbar 1400) and is not among MC-071's
  failures; and `tuning` holds `toongod` entries from the same months whose
  viewport the stage locates at 115..1399 or 115..1400. So whatever makes
  `n06` fail is in `n06`'s content, not in where its bars are.

**Before RED, the Lead PO measures** (once MC-072 and MC-074 are DONE):
`viewport::locate` beside the pipeline's page column for `n06` and `n02`,
and, if it declines, which of the stage's own conditions refuses (its row
profile at the browser-bar end and at the scrollbar and taskbar starts). If
`n02`'s rows are already right after MC-074, AC-2 is dropped before RED.
If [MC-076](MC-076.md)'s `n13` turns out to fail by the same refusal, the two
stories **merge** (re-cut before RED, the user told); otherwise they stay
apart. Their symptoms differ today: `n13`'s crop starts at row 40 and ends
exactly on `B`.

### Measured (2026-10-02, before RED, on `main` at `6af092f`)

The Lead PO measured with a probe in a scratch worktree outside the
repository (the shipped crates with `viewport`'s private helpers made public,
and a test that prints per-row and per-column shares). Tone 11, tolerance 10.

**`n06` is a split-screen screenshot, and the stage declines.** The page
column is `698,0 400x1440` (exact, as MC-071 recorded); `viewport::locate`
beside it is **`None`**. Per column, over rows 115..1392, the share within
tolerance of the tone is **1.00** on 0..697 and 1098..1798, and **0.00** from
1799 to the image's edge (2560): a **second browser window**, textured over
every row, with no flat viewport of its own. So the whole-margin share is
**0.648 on every viewport row** (left side 1.000, right side 0.479), never
reaches `PAGE_LIKE` (0.90), and MC-048's reading finds no run at all. That
reading alone decides whether the stage speaks (MC-052's design: "wherever
MC-048's stage declined, this one declines"), so the reader-window reading,
which would widen it, never runs. Read over only the reader's window
(`reader_window` over 115..1392 is 0..1799), the share is 1.000 on 1255 of
those 1277 rows and at least 0.9 on the other 22. Rows 0..114 and from 1392
down are 0.000 to 0.006 either way.

This is **candidate A**, with the cause named: a second window that,
unlike `f18`'s (`2025-03-06 12_48_06.png`), has no flat rows at all. `f18`'s
whole-margin share is 0.63 to 0.77 on most rows, but 74 rows reach 0.9, a run
of 16 exists, and the window reading widens it to 115..1399. `n06`'s never
gets there.

**`n13` is a different cause, so MC-076 stays apart.** `Screenshot (2507).png`
(tuning since MC-072): page column `977,40 600x1352`, `locate` `None`, tone 22.
Its whole-margin share is **0.889** on its viewport rows (left 0.893, right
0.886), just under 0.90, because of reader panels of another tone at 872..976
and 1577..1681 that are flat on no row. There is no second window, and its
reader window beside the column is empty (977..1577), so no window reading
could recover it. One fix does not cover both.

**`n02`** is done: MC-074's AC-1 put its rows at 115..1399. AC-2 is dropped
below.

**Three trial rules, a warning for GREEN, not a design.** Each was applied in
the scratch worktree only, with the full `--include-ignored` run.
1. *On a whole-margin decline, read the reader's window over every row and
   take its outer runs* (then MC-054's chrome check). `n06` comes out
   115..1392. But `2025-08-05 00_11_13.webp` (column `953,18 640x1422`, window
   952..1597, a 1-column and a 4-column sliver) gets rows **0..76** and is cut
   to them, and `tests/detect.rs`'s
   `the_margin_is_read_from_the_tuning_it_was_given` fails on a generated
   screen (h 86 → 83, 94 → 87). This is exactly the hazard MC-048's
   amendments and MC-052's module notes describe.
2. *The same, with a row page-like only when each side of the column, read
   alone inside the window, is page-like.* The generator passes, but the
   WebP still goes to 0..76.
3. *The same as 2, used only when each side of the window is at least 16
   columns wide* (MC-052 GREEN's removed `MIN_WINDOW_MARGIN`, which existed
   for exactly these two WebPs). **Only `n06` moved:** `698,115 400x1277` at
   margin 0, `695,115 406x1277` at margin 3, rows exactly MC-071's `T` and
   `B`. The only failures were the two expected ones (`n06` in
   `corpus_tuning_crops_unmoved.rs`'s `MAIN_CROPS` and in
   `corpus_viewport_stage.rs`'s declines). Every other suite was green,
   generator included.

So a rule exists that moves only `n06`. Trial 3 is evidence, not the design:
GREEN chooses the rule and justifies any constant it uses, and "16 because
it made `n06` pass" is the failure the success condition names. That
constant is MC-052's own number for the WebPs, measured before `n06` existed.

**What this reverses, in plain words.** The stage's documented rule is "if
the whole-margin reading finds nothing, give up; no second reading after
that". MC-048's user-approved amendment kept the two WebPs' crops exactly as
they were. A fallback that leaves them exactly as they were keeps to that
ruling. The module documentation's "a decline of the whole margin is final"
changes, and GREEN rewrites it.
