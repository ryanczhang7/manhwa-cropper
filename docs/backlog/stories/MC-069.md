## Context

**The report (2026-10-01).** The user ran the app on `Downloads\mahwa panels`
and it answered **`Ambiguous`** (left the screenshot uncropped) on 13 of them,
listed by the user with a screenshot of the app's results: `Screenshot (N).png`
for N = 14, 19, 20, 23, 42, 48, 49, 50, 51, 52, 53, 57, 58. The user added
`Screenshot (2705).png` (its original is gone from the folder; the app's
unchanged copy `Screenshot (2705) (2).png`, 2560x1440, 1,204,928 bytes, is
the same size as the original the MC-068 scan read before the run). The user
asked for these in a fix story, **not** in MC-068's held-out set, because they
were chosen by the app failing on them.

**What is known, from names, sizes and the corpus only** (no image of the 14
has been viewed or measured):
- All 14 are 2560x1440 by header, in `Downloads\mahwa panels`, and none is in
  the corpus.
- ~~Screenshots 14 to 58 are one reading session~~ **Corrected by the user's
  marks (2026-10-01):** they are two readers, not one session. Screenshots
  14, 19, 20, 23 and 42 are `toongod` (dark), and 48 to 58 are
  `demonicrevolution` (light). Neither is `manhwaclan`, the reader of the
  corpus's `Screenshot (56).png` and `Screenshot (59).png`, which sit just
  inside the ambiguity band's edge (at `ambiguity_band` 0.005 they go
  `Ambiguous`, pinned by `corpus.rs`'s `AMBIGUOUS_PAST_THE_CLIFF`;
  `Screenshot (56).png`'s 0.00265363 is the binding threshold). The
  Screenshot numbers are close, but the numbering is the Windows screenshot
  counter, shared across sites.
- **Candidate cause, not a finding:** `content_box` judges a side strip
  `NearlyChrome` (just inside the ambiguity band) and the decision becomes
  `Ambiguous`, as it would for (56) and (59) at a wider band. Nothing ties
  these 14 to (56) and (59) beyond the shared symptom. Whether it holds, and
  on which strip, is RED's to show, per reader.
- `Screenshot (2705).png` is a different session (near the corpus's
  `Screenshot (2698).jpg` and `(2708).jpg`); its cause is not assumed to be
  the same.
- **Which build** the user ran is not recorded. RED first reproduces each
  `Ambiguous` on `main`. One that `main` already crops is still added, as a
  regression entry, and the story says so.

## Acceptance criteria

Re-cut 2026-10-01 on the user's three answers (`## Amendments`). Both
margins means 0 and 3. "The 13" are the 14 less `Screenshot (2705).png`.

**The rule this story ships** (the user's answer, "Ignore if crop excludes
it"): a strip that is *nearly* chrome makes the **decision** `Ambiguous` only
if the final crop rect includes at least one of that strip's pixels. A close
call on a strip the crop lies wholly outside of changes nothing in the answer
and does not flag. The strip-level judgement itself (`content_box`, and
`ContentBox::ambiguous`) is unchanged; only what the decision makes of it
narrows.

- **AC-1: the files.** The 14 are copied into `fixtures/corpus/` as
  `tuning` entries, under their original names, byte-identical to the
  originals (SHA-256 in `## Notes`), each with the user's frozen mark as
  amended for `Screenshot (42).png` (`1073,167 400x1225`), site and gap tags.
  The corpus stays under its 120 MiB cap (it lands at 125,211,400 bytes).
- **AC-2: the reproduction.** On `main`, each of the 13 is processed at both
  margins and answers `Flag(Ambiguous)`. A test asserting each is `Cropped`
  containing its mark is red on `main` for exactly that reason.
- **AC-3: cropped, no clip.** After the fix, each of the 13 is `Cropped` at
  both margins and contains its mark. `Screenshot (2705).png` is a named
  known exception: it stays `Flag(Ambiguous)` at both margins, its fix is
  MC-070, and the list naming it is exact in both directions.
- **AC-4: nothing else moves.** Every other marked `tuning` crop is exactly
  what `main` produces at both margins, every flag entry stays flagged, and
  zero clips holds everywhere. The one measured exception: `2025-07-17
  14_20_23.png` (MC-056's `KNOWN_AMBIGUOUS`) now crops - its close call is the
  same scrollbar - and must contain its mark at both margins; it leaves that
  list and `Screenshot (2705).png` enters it. The corpus control that the
  band sits just below a real cliff (`AMBIGUOUS_PAST_THE_CLIFF`) is kept and
  measured at the strip judgement (`content_box`), where the band and its
  cliff live; there it stays exactly the five entries, plus any new `tuning`
  entry that turns there, measured and named in `## Notes`.
- **AC-5: a generated reproduction** in the required `unit` gate: a page
  whose side strip falls just inside the ambiguity band, wholly outside the
  page the crop keeps, answers `Crop` (red on `main`, which flags it); its
  control, the same nearly-chrome strip lying where the crop includes it,
  still answers `Flag(Ambiguous)`.
