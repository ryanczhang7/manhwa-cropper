## Context

**The bug.** MC-048's viewport stage cuts the reader's artwork on
split-screen screenshots. It reads the browser chrome and the taskbar of a
*second* window beside the reader as if they were the reader's own. The crop
then clips the art at the top, the bottom, or both. **The user's decision,
2026-09-24: "Fix story, top priority".** It runs before MC-053.

**How it was found.** MC-048 shipped without its held-out check.
(MC-048 `## Amendments`, "Open question 2 — the held-out run is not made".)
The first aggregate held-out run after it was merged, by the orchestrator on
2026-09-24, on `main` at `26eddcb` (release build, headless), read:

| Build | Marked held-out | Cropped | Not cropped | Clips | Row sides |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0f94c75` (before MC-048) | 24 | 23 | 1 | 4 | none |
| `26eddcb` (MC-048) | 24 | 21 | 3 | 7 | `bottom:103 bottom:31 bottom:2 top:3` |

The column sides (`left:3 left:6 right:2 right:5 right:5`) are the same on
both builds. They are MC-053's and are out of scope here. With the user's
approval, the orchestrator then looked at the row-clipped edges:

- **`2025-03-06 01_22_45.png`**: top cut by 3 rows and bottom by 103.
- **`2025-03-07 00_58_06.png`**: bottom cut by 31 rows.
- **`2025-09-29 14_33_15.png`**: bottom 2 rows. This is a **mark error**, not
  a clip. The user ruled on a labelled render: rows 1400–1401 are taskbar, and
  the art ends at row 1399. Whole-screen mean brightness per row is ≈ 57 over
  1396–1400 and ≈ 62.4 from 1401.

The first two are split-screen screenshots. The reader window is on the left,
from about x 0 to 1828. A second browser window (YouTube) is on the right. It
has its own bookmark bar, which ends lower, and its own bottom edge, which is
higher.

**The mechanism, measured by the Lead PO on 2026-09-24 against `26eddcb`.** It
confirms the orchestrator's hypothesis. `viewport::locate`
(`crates/core/src/viewport.rs`) scores each row by the share of **every pixel
outside the page column, across the full image width**, that is page
background. A row is page-like at 0.90 or above. On these two files:

| Rows (`01_22_45`) | Left of column | Right of column, inside the reader | Second window (x ≥ 1828) | Full width, as `locate` reads it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 104–114 (reader chrome) | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |
| 115–120 | 0.988 | 0.974 | **0.000** (its bookmark bar) | **0.627** |
| 121–1258 | 0.988–0.998 | 0.974 | 0.964–0.975 | 0.975–0.982 |
| 1259–1373 | 0.988 | 0.963–0.974 | **0.000** (its bottom panel) | **0.623–0.627** |
| 1374–1399 (below the reader window) | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |
| 1400– (taskbar) | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |

`00_58_06` reads the same. The reader window is page-like over 115–1398, and
the second window over 121–1258. The full-width share on the rows only the
reader covers is 0.634–0.637. So `locate` returns `Viewport { top: 121,
bottom: 1259 }` on **both** files. That is the second window's viewport, and
the crop's rows are clamped to it. The reader's own viewport is rows
**115..1374** on `01_22_45` and **115..1399** on `00_58_06`. (These are the
probe's numbers, from its own tone estimate. `## Model guidance` says what RED
does with them.)

**What the user decided on 2026-09-24:**

1. The two real-clip entries (`2025-03-06 01_22_45.png` and
   `2025-03-07 00_58_06.png`) **move from `held-out` to `tuning`**. They
   reproduce the bug, and the fix is designed while looking at them. That
   leaves 22 marked held-out entries.
2. **`2025-09-29 14_33_15.png`'s mark is corrected**: `h` 1231 → 1229, so the
   rect is `984,171 576x1229` (rows 171..1399). It stays `held-out`.
3. **Zero clips is required on both sets.** On held-out, the orchestrator
   checks it at GATES in one aggregate run, counts only (AC-7). MC-048 skipped
   that run and shipped this bug. **This story must not skip it.**

**The required gates that fail if this breaks.** `unit` fails through AC-6,
the synthetic two-window screenshot. `integration` fails through AC-1 to AC-5,
the corpus. That is why `integration` is in `required_gates`.

### Anything that changes the approach

- **The fixture has a window edge, deliberately.** The second window's
  viewport is exactly the page tone, as the corpus's nearly is (0.964-0.975
  flat). Between the windows sits the reader's 17 px scrollbar (value 180, over
  the reader's viewport rows only), as on the corpus (x 1811..1827). A fix may
  read that edge or not; the fixture does not forbid either. Without it, only
  per-row reasoning could pass case A, which would have constrained the fix
  beyond the ACs.
- **The either-side rule is a real temptation.** P1 shows it passes case A and
  every single-window synthetic scene - only case B and the corpus WebPs catch
  it. Expect the fix to need a notion of "which window's pixels", not just
  "which side".
- **Row 115..120 on the corpus two are reader-only on top.** Those six rows
  carry the mark's top on `01_22_45` (mark top 118). The fix has to take them.
- `22_46_15` stays a MISS in the 90% count by design (flag expected, cropped
  `72,3 225x710`); 28 of 30 is the ceiling this story can reach.

   viewport, not over every row.** On a split-screen shot those are the rows
   both windows cover, and there the reader's margin is flat and its scrollbar
   is not. On a border-row viewport every column is flat, so the window is the
   whole margin and nothing widens.
