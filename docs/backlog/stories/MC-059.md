## Context

works on any reader."

[MC-051](MC-051.md) took `EPIC-07`'s one held-out score at `d2876f5`
([`held-out-score.md`](../../wiki/held-out-score.md)): **0 clips on 19 of 19,
and 16 of 19 met the bar against 18 required**, so the bar is not met. Two of
the three misses are a site's own header left inside the crop:

- `Screenshot (68).png` (`kunmanga`): the site's header, rows 167..319, is
  painted in the page's own background tone, so MC-048's viewport stage reads
  it as page and keeps it. This is the failure MC-050 predicted
  ([`reader-furniture-search.md`](../../wiki/reader-furniture-search.md) §1,
  §6): on tuning, MC-048's crop left reader furniture on 2 of 21 entries, both
  `kunmanga`'s same-tone header ending at row 293.
- `2025-08-03 11_27_49.png` (`rolia-scans`): the user ruled **one row** of site
  header (115..116) directly under the browser bar. The ruling's precision at
  one row is doubtful (MC-051 `## Notes`, the ruling passes), and MC-051 §1
  shows the verdict does not rest on it.

The third miss, `2025-07-17 14_20_23.png` (`xbato`), is the detector
declining the page (`Ambiguous`). That is a separate question and not this
story's.

`held-out-score.md` §6 names the next step: a furniture rule for same-tone site
headers, fitted on tuning plus the three entries MC-051 read, that **must still
work on any reader**. [MC-056](MC-056.md) has since moved the three to
`tuning`: **36 tuning entries, 29 of them marked** — `toongod` 13,
`demonicrevolution` 4, `kunmanga` 4, `xbato` 3, `rolia-scans` 3, `w-network` 2.

### Why this is a spike and not a feature

A feature story's criteria assert a bar, and a story must not assert a bar no
rule has been shown to meet (`story-authoring`; MC-032 `## Closed` is what
happened when six stories did). **No furniture rule has ever been scored.**
MC-050 stopped at its AC-2 before scoring any rule family, and its §5 says in
terms that its evidence supports "MC-048 already meets the bar on these 21",
**not** "a reader-agnostic furniture rule exists". So there is no measured
rule for a feature to implement and no evidence that the bar is reachable
without per-site knowledge.

The honest order is **spike, then feature**. This spike answers whether such a
rule exists on the tuning set and survives having each reader held out of its
own fitting. If it does, its `## Notes` sketch the feature story, which then
implements a measured rule against numbers this spike established. If it does
not, the reasoned negative says which decision that forces. Nothing is lost by
the order: the feature would have to run this search in RED anyway, with
frozen criteria it might not be able to meet.

### What "works on any reader" can and cannot mean on this evidence

The brief (§5, the amendments of 2026-09-21) makes "any reader" binding:
per-site matching may only ever be an optimisation on top of a rule that works
without it ([MC-041](MC-041.md), parked). A rule that has learned
`kunmanga`'s header height does not answer this spike.

**Only one reader in the tuning set has a same-tone header: `kunmanga`**, with
4 marked entries (`Screenshot (67)`, `(68)`, `(70)` and, unruled so far,
`(73)`). Every other reader's crop has no site header inside it, except the
one-row `rolia-scans` line. So:

- **What can be shown:** that a rule whose parameters were chosen **without
  seeing any `kunmanga` entry** removes `kunmanga`'s header without clipping,
  and that the same rule does not clip or misfire on the five other readers.
  That is the leave-one-reader-out check (AC-4), and its `kunmanga` fold is the
  only fold that tests *finding* a header. The other five folds test only that
  the rule does no harm.
- **What cannot be shown here:** that it works on a *different* site's
  same-tone header. One reader's header design is one example. "Any reader" is
  finally tested by **new screenshots**, which is also what `EPIC-07`'s owed
  score needs (an MC-037-shaped story). This spike's positive verdict is
  therefore "a candidate that survives the only generalisation test the tuning
  set can run", never "works on any reader".
- **What a negative means:** if no rule finds `kunmanga`'s header with
  `kunmanga` held out, the tuning set cannot justify a reader-agnostic rule,
  whatever the in-sample score.

### The `Screenshot (67).png` conflict is the user's to resolve

On `Screenshot (67).png` the mark starts at row **290** and the user ruled the
site header's end at row **293** (MC-050 §3). Cutting the ruled header clips
the mark by 3 rows, so under zero clips a rule **may not** remove the last 3
rows of that header, and that entry cannot be furniture-free by the exact test.
Either the site's bar is drawn over the top of the picture, or the mark is 3
rows too tall. This spike **does not decide which** (Open question 2). Until
the user rules otherwise it is a known miss and the pass line below counts it
as one.

## Acceptance criteria

Every number is over the **marked `tuning` entries** and nothing else. The
harness filters on `split == "tuning"` and a rectangle `expect` **before a file
is named or decoded**, and asserts that 29 remain. No held-out entry is opened,
decoded, listed or re-scored, not even as a labelled re-score.

**The scored set** is those 29 minus `2025-07-17 14_20_23.png` (the detector
declines it; out of scope): **28 entries**. That entry's result is reported on
its own line and counts toward nothing.

**Furniture-free** is MC-051's exact test on the reader part only: with `T` the
ruled site-header end (or the crop's current top edge where the header is
"none") and `B` the ruled site-footer start (likewise), `crop.y >= T` and
`crop.y + crop.h <= B`, exact to the row. Browser chrome and the taskbar are
MC-048's and not scored here (the two WebPs' chrome is MC-048's named
limitation).

**The pass line** is the brief's 9 in 10, rounded up: **0 clips on 28 of 28
(absolute) and furniture-free on at least 26 of 28**. How the one-row
`rolia-scans` line is counted is Open question 3; the default counts both ways
and applies the pass line to "set aside". The figures are recomputed and
written into the document from the AC-1 oracle **before any rule is scored**
(AC-2); if an answer to an Open question moves the denominator, the pass line
moves with it by the same 9-in-10 rounding, and that is recorded in
`## Amendments` before RED ends.

- **AC-1** — *The oracle, completed.* Given the 5 marked tuning entries with no
  furniture ruling, when the user has ruled them on the page described in
  `## Context` and the answers are read back with `ArtifactData`, then the
  document carries one table of all 29 marked tuning entries with, per entry:
  header end, footer start, the source (MC-050 round 2, MC-051 frozen, or this
  story), and who ruled it and when. *Check that fails if broken:* the harness
  refuses to score (exits non-zero, naming the entry) while any of the 29 lacks
  a ruling, and its row count equals 29.
- **AC-2** — *The baseline and the ceiling, before any rule.* Given the AC-1
  oracle and `main`'s crop at `Tuning::default()`, then the document reports,
  over the 28 and per reader: (a) the **identity rule** (today's crop, no new
  stage): clips and furniture-free count; (b) the **ceiling** (crop cut exactly
  to the ruled rows, current edge where "none"): clips, which is expected to be
  1 (`Screenshot (67).png`, 3 rows) unless Open question 2 changes it, and the
  largest furniture-free count reachable with 0 clips. Expected, to be
  measured and not assumed: identity 23 to 25 of 28 (depending on
  `Screenshot (73).png`'s ruling and Open question 3), zero-clip ceiling 27 of
  28. *Early stop:* if the identity rule already meets the pass line, the spike
  stops here and says so, as MC-050 did.
- **AC-3** — *At most two rule families, reader-agnostic by construction.* For
  each family: its definition, the parameters swept, and a **selection rule
  written into the document before the first score**: 0 clips first, then most
  furniture-free, then **fewest rows of plain page trimmed** on entries ruled
  "no site header" (the tie-break that stops a rule winning by cutting page).
  For the selected member, the in-sample counts over the 28: clips,
  furniture-free, and plain-page rows trimmed, per edge and per reader. *Out,
  by construction:* any per-site constant or lookup, a reader or file name
  anywhere in the rule, colour (chroma), and any learned model. A family whose
  selected member does not reach the pass line in-sample is reported with its
  numbers and failure causes by entry, and the spike does not widen the sweep.
- **AC-4** — *Leave one reader out.* For each family that reached the pass
  line in-sample, its parameters are re-selected by AC-3's rule with each
  reader's entries withheld (`toongod` 13, `demonicrevolution` 4, `kunmanga` 4,
  `xbato` 3, `rolia-scans` 3, `w-network` 2; 14_20_23 is out, so `xbato`
  folds on 2 of the 28) and scored on that reader alone. The document reports
  each fold and the sum over the 28. **The `kunmanga` fold is reported first
  and on its own**, since it is the only fold whose fitting set contains no
  same-tone header. *Two controls, each shown firing:*
  - **Memorising control:** a rule that stores one fixed header height per
    reader, fitted in-sample, must score at or near the ceiling in-sample and
    **fail** the `kunmanga` fold (it has no height for a reader it has not
    seen, so it falls back to trimming nothing).
  - **Blind-offset control:** the single fixed number of rows trimmed below
    the crop's top on every entry, the largest that keeps 0 clips in-sample,
    must fail the pass line. Expected: at most 1 row (`Screenshot (2744).jpg`'s
    mark starts 1 row below its crop), which removes no `kunmanga` header.
- **AC-5** — *The scorer's own controls, shown firing.* The ruled rows moved
  10 rows inward must report at least one clip (the clip check is live); the
  identity rule must report furniture on exactly the entries whose ruling names
  a site bar inside today's crop (the absence check is live). Both outputs are
  pasted into the document.
- **AC-6** — *The verdict.* Exactly one of:
  - **A candidate rule exists:** a family's member reaches 0 clips on 28 of 28
    and at least 26 of 28 furniture-free **both in-sample and summed over the
    AC-4 folds**, and in the `kunmanga` fold it is furniture-free on **more**
    `kunmanga` entries than the identity rule is. The document states what that
    does and does not establish (`## Context`, the section on what "works on any
    reader" can and cannot mean), and `## Notes` sketches the feature story it justifies: the
    stage and where it sits in `architecture.md`'s pipeline, its criteria as
    the counts measured here, `required_gates`, and that its first score on
    unseen screenshots needs **new** screenshots, never a re-score of the spent
    set.
  - **A reasoned negative:** the ceiling, each family's best numbers in-sample
    and under AC-4, and the failure causes by entry, with the decision it
    forces named for the user (for example: more captures of sites with
    same-tone headers before any rule is fitted; opening per-site matching,
    which the brief allows only on top of a general rule; or learned
    detection). Choosing among them is the user's decision, not this spike's.
- **AC-7** — *The record.* The document is `docs/wiki/site-header-search.md`;
  `docs/wiki/v2-candidates.md` gains a row; every number is reproducible from a
  harness **outside the repository** whose path, commands and source hash the
  document records, as MC-050 §7 does. The document states that no held-out
  entry was opened, and the harness's tuning filter is the evidence.

  furniture (Open question 3). The spike neither proposes nor pre-fills the
  new rulings.
- **Oracle-free — the work.** The two rule families, their parameters, and the
  selection rule (AC-3), each held to the controls of AC-4 and AC-5, which
  must fire hard: the memorising control must fail the `kunmanga` fold
  outright, not narrowly.
- **Mechanical — pin exactly.** Denominators (29 filtered, 28 scored, per
  reader), the furniture-free test (MC-051's, exact to the row), the report
  shape, AC-7.

**Success condition, which can come out either way.** A reader of
`site-header-search.md` can say, with numbers, whether a rule with no per-site
knowledge finds `kunmanga`'s header with `kunmanga` withheld from its fitting,
without clipping or misfiring on the other five readers, and what that does
and does not show about other sites. It **fails** if it scores any rule before
AC-2's ceiling and pass line are written down, chooses a selection rule after
seeing scores, reports any number over a denominator other than the marked
tuning entries, reads a held-out entry, or states a positive verdict as "works
on any reader". **Record the verdict in `## Notes`.**

**Timebox.** AC-1 and AC-2 first. Two families at most. If neither selected
member reaches the pass line in-sample, write the negative without a wider
sweep.

**Families, as examples only** (the spike chooses, within AC-3's limits): a
full-width horizontal divider below the viewport's top edge with only small,
text-sized marks above it; or a band at the viewport top made only of small
connected components (menu text, buttons) that ends before the first component
that reaches into the page column. Both are 2D readings, which is the opening
`EPIC-07`'s *Why now* names; neither is a row statistic of the kind MC-032
closed.
