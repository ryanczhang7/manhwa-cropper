### Why only two bottoms need ruling

MC-071's reasoning, unchanged:
- **Tops:** every one of the 10 marks starts on a browser-bar end (115 or
  167), so `T` is the mark's top and there is no site header above it.
- **Bottoms:** where a mark ends on its group's usual bottom row, the art
  touches the taskbar, so `B` is the mark's bottom.
- **The only unknown** is the band below a mark that stops short: page
  background, which a crop may keep, or furniture (MC-071 ruled 1392 the
  scrollbar on `toongod`'s `n06` and `n10`; the user ruled the scrollbar
  browser furniture).

Grouped by top row, from the frozen marks (counts and ids only):

| 167 | 1400 (all 3) | none |
| 115 | 1400 (5 of 7) | `n06` 1392, `n10` 1392 |

So **8 are read from the mark and 2 are ruled.** For the 8, an off-by-one in
a mark would score as a clip or as furniture present and would not be caught

## Definitions used by every criterion

MC-071's, with the denominators changed:

- **Scored commit**: the `main` commit (or a story-branch commit whose
  `crates/`, `Cargo.toml` and `Cargo.lock` are identical to `main`'s) whose
  release build is run, recorded with the harness's source hash **before**
  AC-3 runs. `git diff 4539e12 <scored commit> -- crates/core
  crates/engine/src crates/app/src Cargo.lock --stat` is pasted beside it.
- **The crop**: `process_file`'s outcome at `Tuning::default()` (margin_px 0,
  the app's own setting) on the scored commit, release build. For `Cropped {
  rect }`, rows `rect.y .. rect.y + rect.h` (bottom exclusive). For
  `Flagged`, the whole image. Margin 3 is not scored (the user, 2026-10-03).
- **Clip**: the crop does not contain the marked rect on all four sides.
- **`T`**: the browser-bar end (the mark's top on all 10). **`B`**: the
  smallest of the scrollbar start, the site-footer start and the taskbar
  start, the image height if none.
- **Furniture-free**: `crop.y >= T` and `crop.y + crop.h <= B`, exact to the
  row.
- **Cause attribution**, fixed before any crop: browser chrome if `crop.y <
  T`; browser scrollbar if the crop's bottom is past `scrollbarStart`; reader
  footer if past `footerStart`; taskbar if past `taskbarStart`; flagged if
  the outcome is `Flagged`.
- **A flagged entry** is scored as the whole image: no clip, furniture
  present, does **not** meet the bar.
- **Meets the bar**: no clip and furniture-free. The bar is **0 clips on 10
  of 10** (absolute) **and at least 9 of 10** meeting it (the user,
  2026-10-03: nine in ten of 10 is exactly 9).
