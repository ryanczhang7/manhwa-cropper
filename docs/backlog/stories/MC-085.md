## Context

[MC-084](MC-084.md) put 10 screenshots, drawn blind from the Eleceed folder
and marked by the user, into `held-out`. This spike spends them: it runs the
crop **once** over the 10 and records `EPIC-07`'s bar on them in
`docs/wiki/held-out-score.md`. It is [MC-078](MC-078.md)'s shape, and MC-078
is the template: its harness, its controls and its write-up.

**Filed 2026-10-05, on the user's word: "you can create a story for it but
dont run it".** It is not started. The user reports that the screenshots they
run the app on work. That is not a score: their runs are not checked against
their marks pixel by pixel. This story is.

**The set** (MC-084, `docs/wiki/corpus.md`, "The fourth fresh held-out set"):
10 marked, 0 flag, **one reader, `toongod`**, dark pages, mid-chapter,
2560x1440. Every mark starts at row 115 (the browser bar's end). Nine end at
1400. **`2025-03-04 18_37_39.png` (`h06`) ends at 1374**, so where its
bottom furniture starts is not read from its mark. AC-1 handles this, as
MC-078 handled `n06` and `n10`.

**The scored detector** is `main` after MC-082 and MC-083, both of which
changed `flat::locate_column`. MC-078's score was of `c108bd1`, before
MC-080 to MC-083.

**What a score here can speak for:** crops on `toongod` only, mid-chapter,
dark pages. It says nothing about any other reader, an unseen reader, a
screenshot that should be left alone, or a light page. With 10 entries each
miss is 10 points, so the 90 % bar allows exactly one.

**The gate that would fail if this broke:** `integration`, as for MC-078.

    AC-1's rule). The screenshot is copied server-side from *Eleceed

- **AC-2** — *Before any crop touches the 10*, each with its output in
  `## Notes`, committed before the run:
  - (a) oracle against mark on `h06`: a conflict is put to the user by name;
  - (b) the harness reproduces **MC-078's aggregate exactly** on its 10, now
    `tuning`, built against `c108bd1` with MC-077's frozen marks (so `n05`
    is `973,167 600x1233`): 1 clip of 10 (column), meets 9 of 10, failing
    entry `n05` only. Labelled "a reproduction on spent entries, not a
    score";
  - (c) controls on the fresh oracle, with no crop run: the whole image as
    the "crop" reports furniture on 10 of 10, and each mark less 10 rows top
    and bottom reports a clip on 10 of 10;
  - (d) no suite has cropped the 10: the greps for `held-out` and for each

- **AC-3** — *One run*, MC-078 AC-3's harness rules unchanged:
  - it reports clips (row and column), meets the bar against 9, reader

    marked entries answered `Flagged`, and the per-reader line;

  - per-file lines are written for failing entries only, after the totals.
- **AC-4** — *The verdict*, exactly one of:
  - **met**: recorded as "meets the bar on sites it knows, at this version",
    with the goal kept open;
  - **not met, 0 clips**: failures by cause, the next stories named but not
    filed;
  - **a clip**: stop and ask before anything else.

## Open questions

Put these to the user in plain words before the story starts. Recommended
answers are MC-078's, which the user chose on 2026-10-03:
1. **The pass mark:** 9 of 10; any cut into the art fails outright; an
   uncropped screenshot is a miss; only the default margin is scored.
2. **`h06`'s bottom:** rule it on a page showing just its bottom strip,
   three lines, none of the app's.
3. **Afterwards:** totals for all 10 first, then each failure's cause; on
   any clip, stop and ask; passing screenshots stay unexamined.
4. **If it passes:** record "meets the bar on sites it knows, at this
   version"; the goal stays open until there is evidence on a reader the
   cropper has never seen.
