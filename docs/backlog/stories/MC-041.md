### Why `spike` and not `feature`

The epic says *"Spike or feature, depending on 2"*. Story 2 has reported, so
the decision is made here, and it is **`spike`** — output is a document,
PLANNED → REVIEW → DONE with no RED, as MC-028, MC-031, MC-034, MC-035 and
MC-038 all ran (`story-authoring`, "Types"). Four reasons, in descending
weight:

- **A `feature` here would have to assert a bar, and the only bar this
  signal can currently reach is the one the user declined.** MC-031 §9 Option B
  proposed asserting *"no output contains browser or OS chrome"* instead of an
  accuracy bar, backed by an oracle that never clips and lies outside the mark
  on **19 of 19 entries where it speaks**. On **2026-09-17**, shown the crop it
  produces for `Screenshot (67).png`, the user's answer was **no — the site's
  own navigation is also unacceptable** (`architecture.md` decision 14's
  amendment; `EPIC-07` `## Why now`). A furniture matcher that removes chrome
  *and* the site's navigation is Option B with one more strip peeled off. It
  may be **an internal stage of a rule that goes further**; it may **not**
  become the product's bar. So the shippable criterion for a feature story does
  not exist yet, and inventing one is precisely what MC-032's `## Closed`
  records going wrong.
- **The premise has never been measured.** "A site's navigation bar is
  pixel-identical across every screenshot from that site" is an assertion in
  the epic and in the brief amendment. Nothing in this repository has checked
  it, and there are obvious ways for it to be false in part: a nav bar carrying
  the series title, a scroll-position-dependent sticky header, a different
  window size or browser zoom, a light/dark toggle. AC-1 is that measurement,
  and it is cheap and decisive enough to end the story on its own.
- **The tuning set carries no site labels at all** (next section). Any story
  that assumed it could group tuning screenshots by reader would be wrong on
  its first day. Establishing what *can* be measured, and by what protocol, is
  investigation work.
- **A feature would not fit one RED → GREEN cycle**, and the line is named
  below so nobody has to invent it under pressure.

**Sizing, and where the line would be if this were a feature.** The feature
this spike could recommend is at minimum: a furniture-template representation,
a matcher over it, a compiled-in library of templates (`architecture.md`
decision 11 — no settings pane and no tuning file, so the library ships in the
binary), the fallback path for an unmatched reader, and the wiring into
`decide`. That is four or five behaviours, each demonstrable on its own — the
`story-authoring` "too big" test fires on every one of its signs. **The split
line, if a feature is ever recommended, is between *recognising* furniture and
*acting* on it:** one story that reports a matched furniture extent and nothing
else, and a second that moves the crop edge to it. Do not reserve ids for them
here — they may never exist, and a reserved id that goes unused is a gap the
next author has to explain.

**As a spike this fits one story**, and the comparison that settles it is
MC-038: two families, 1120 parameterisations, ten harness binaries, four
negative controls, one document. This story's instrument is *cheaper* than
that — cross-image agreement is a scan, not a labelling — and its worst case is
much smaller than its best case, because AC-1 and AC-2 come before any sweep
and `## Model guidance`'s timebox stops the story at either of them. The
expensive path is only taken when the ceiling says it is worth taking. If AC-1
and AC-2 consume the budget, **stop, write the document with AC-1, AC-2 and
AC-6 answered and the sweep explicitly unrun, and file the sweep as its own
story with the harness's location recorded** — that is MC-038's own split rule
and it applies unchanged.

### The corpus problem this story has and MC-038 did not

**No entry in the tuning set carries a `site:` tag. All 31 of them are on
held-out entries.** Verified against `fixtures/corpus/manifest.json` on
2026-09-21: 59 entries, 28 `tuning` / 31 `held-out`; **0 tuning entries carry a
`site:` tag and all 31 held-out entries carry exactly one** — `toongod` 10,
`rolia-scans` 8, `w-network` 5, `xbato` 3, `manhwaclan` 2, `kunmanga` 2,
`demonicrevolution` 1.

That is by design, not by omission. `corpus.md`, "The `site:` tags":
*"Pre-EPIC-07 entries carry none and are not required to"* — attributing a
reader to a screenshot captured a year earlier *"is exactly the situation that
manufactures guesses, and this page already rules that a guessed tag is worse
than an absent one"*. What the user gave instead, on **2026-09-18**, is
`PRE_EPIC_07_SITES` as a **set**: the 28 came from **`toongod`,
`rolia-scans`, `w-network` and `xbato`**, with no per-file attribution.

Three consequences, and they shape every criterion below:

- **This story may not attribute them.** Re-tagging is out of scope, and
  `corpus.md` is explicit that it is a person's call. The spike works with an
  unlabelled tuning set.
- **That is not the obstacle it first looks like, because the product has no
  labels either.** The brief amendment: *"Furniture is recognised from the
  pixels in front of it; nothing is fetched, and no site is contacted."* At
  run time the detector is handed one PNG and no site name. So the instrument
  this spike needs — **group screenshots by the furniture they share, from the
  pixels alone** — is the same instrument the product would need, and the
  absence of tags forces the honest version of it rather than a shortcut.
- **It sharpens MC-038 §9b's second caution rather than answering it.** That
  caution reads: *"`corpus.md` records the site tags MC-037 added across seven
  readers with counts as low as one; a furniture matcher measured on `toongod`
  (10 entries) and `demonicrevolution` (1) is measuring two different
  things."* Those counts are **held-out** counts, and this story does not score
  held-out. On the tuning set the caution becomes stronger and simpler: **no
  per-reader breakdown is possible at all**, so the document must report
  discovered groups and their sizes rather than named readers, and must say
  that a group of one measures nothing. AC-1 requires exactly that.

### The denominator, and the one rule this story must not break

**Every number in the document is measured over the 28 `tuning` entries — 21
marked and 7 `"expect": "flag"`.** Verified against
`fixtures/corpus/manifest.json` on 2026-09-21: 59 entries, **28 `tuning` / 31
`held-out`**; 14 flag entries, **7 in each split**; so held-out is 24 marked and
7 flag.

**The held-out set is not scored here. Not once, not "just to see."** A spike
is tuning work by definition. `docs/wiki/corpus.md`, "The rule, stated so it
cannot be read two ways":

> no threshold is chosen, adjusted or rejected on the strength of a held-out
> result; no per-file table of held-out results is read while a rule is still
> being changed; and an entry that has been used to make any such decision is no
> longer held out, whatever its `split` says. [...] contamination only runs one
> way.

**This story has a contamination route MC-038 did not, and it is the reason
AC-1's guard must cover reads as well as scores.** A furniture matcher is built
from images. Constructing a template out of a held-out screenshot contaminates
it just as thoroughly as scoring it would — more so, because the contamination
would be baked into the rule rather than merely read off it. The guard
`region-row-search.md` 2a describes (pixels reachable only through a
`corpus::Entry`; private fields; `corpus::admit` returns `Err(Refusal)` for a
`held-out` row **before it has built the file path**) is therefore the minimum
here, and AC-1 requires it to be shown firing on a *template-construction*
call, not only on a scoring call.

The second reason the denominator is fixed is comparability. `corpus.md`: the
`Split::Tuning` filter "keeps **MC-019's, MC-026's and MC-032's recorded numbers
meaning what they meant** [...] without the filter their denominator would
silently have become fifty-nine". A new denominator is a product decision — if
this spike wants one it stops and asks; `## Out of scope` says so.

## Acceptance criteria

The deliverable is one document, `docs/wiki/site-furniture-search.md`, and one
row in `docs/wiki/v2-candidates.md`'s "Already measured — do not re-run these"
table. These criteria are about them.

- **AC-1** — *The epic's premise is measured before anything is built on it, and the measurement is shown not to be vacuous.* Given the document, when its first evidence section is read, then it reports whether **rows of a screenshot are pixel-identical, or near-identical at a stated tolerance, across different screenshots in the tuning set**: the groups of entries it finds that share furniture, each group's size, which rows agree, and at what tolerance; the entries that join no group; and the agreement it finds at the **top** of the image and at the **bottom** separately. Groups are discovered **from the pixels**, never from a `site:` tag — the tuning set has none — and the document says so. *Control, and it is the one that decides whether any of this means anything:* **a flat row agrees with every other flat row**, so agreement measured over uniform rows is vacuous in exactly the way MC-019 `## Notes` measurement 3 was (a statistic decisive at 78.6 % within 0 px, withdrawn when its control scored 99.4 % on all *other* rows). The document must therefore report the same agreement statistic over (i) pairs it groups together, (ii) pairs it does not, and (iii) rows with no structure in them at all, and show that the three separate. If they do not, the premise is reported as **unsupported by measurement**, in those words, and the story ends there with a reasoned negative — which is a complete and valuable outcome.
- **AC-2** — *The ceiling: a perfect furniture oracle is scored against the marks before any rule is tuned, and stated against MC-031's chrome oracle.* Given the document, when its ceiling section is read, then it takes the furniture extent AC-1 discovers as **the answer with no rule error at all** — the crop edges placed exactly at the furniture boundary — and reports, per entry over the **21 marked tuning entries and both edges (42 edges)**, whether that placement lands inside MC-019's 11 px window without clipping, with the three totals **top / bottom / both**, in the per-file shape of `panel-edge-search.md` 2c. Each total is stated beside **MC-031's chrome oracle: 0 of 21 right, 0 clips, top 4 of 21, bottom 2 of 21** (`chrome-row-search.md` §4), and beside the two reachability ceilings, **MC-034's 20 / 11 / 10** and **MC-038's 17 / 13 / 10**. *Controls on the number, both of which must be addressed:* a ceiling of **21 of 21** is the vacuous answer and is rejected — if no entry is excluded, the oracle is reading the mark rather than the furniture, and the document must say how; and a ceiling that **reproduces MC-031 §4's 0 of 21 with the same residual being panel gutter** is reported as *"the site's own furniture is not the residual; MC-031 §4 holds with one more strip peeled"* — which closes the epic's last non-learned idea and is a valid and valuable finding, not a failure to deliver. The document must state, per entry, **how much of MC-031's residual the site's own furniture accounts for** — the distance from the chrome boundary to the furniture boundary, and from the furniture boundary to the mark.
- **AC-3** — *A template built from an entry is never used to score that entry.* Given any score in the document produced by matching furniture, when its protocol section is read, then it states that every template applied to entry X was constructed **without X** — leave-one-out within the tuning set — and names the mechanism that enforces it. *Control, which decides whether the scores mean anything at all:* the document reports the same score **with** the leave-one-out protocol and **without** it, and shows the second is higher. A matcher scored against the image its own template came from is measuring image equality; if the two numbers are the same, the protocol is not doing what it claims and the document says so. It also reports how many entries are left **unscorable** by leave-one-out — an entry whose group has size one has no template at all — and those entries are counted as **not placed**, never silently dropped from the denominator.
- **AC-4** — *The brief's "no worse on an unseen reader" constraint is measured, not assumed.* Given the document, when its fallback section is read, then it reports, over the **7 `"expect": "flag"` tuning entries** and over every marked entry whose furniture matches no template, that the rule **falls back to today's behaviour rather than guessing**: the no-regression baseline is the shipped pipeline's **1 of 7 flag entries cropped** (`region-row-search.md` 6(c), reproducing MC-034's), and cropping more than 1 of 7 is a regression. The brief amendment's words are the criterion: *"A site the matcher does not know must fall back to today's behaviour, not guess — the tool must get no worse on an unseen reader than it is now."* *Control:* the document must exhibit at least one input on which the matcher **declines**, and show that declining produces today's rect rather than a degraded one. A matcher that never declines has no fallback path and is reported as having none.
- **AC-5** — *Both of MC-019's numbers, per entry, plus the clip-free best and the position on the trade-off.* Given the document, when its scoring section is read, then for every rule it scores it reports **how many of the 21 marked tuning entries land inside the 11 px window** and **how many are clipped**, computed over the **final, margin-expanded, clamped** rect as MC-028 §5b, MC-031 §8, MC-034 §6 and MC-038 §5 do, with a **per-entry table**; and it reports the **best clip-free** member separately, because MC-005 decision 13 ranks a clip above every other defect. The result is stated against the baselines in `## Context` in these words: **"beats 8 of 21" or "does not beat 8 of 21"**, and **"the clip-free best is N of 21"** beside MC-028's 1 of 21 and MC-034/MC-035/MC-038's 0 of 21. Finally it answers `v2-candidates.md`'s question explicitly: whether the family **breaks** the clips-versus-placements trade-off or only **moves along** it — a member that both places more *and* clips less than every prior member breaks it; anything else does not. The two `diagonal-gutter` entries are scored **exactly as MC-019 scores them**; the three gutter-crossing entries may additionally be reported under MC-035's disjunction predicate **alongside** the headline and must never be folded into it. It also reports, per entry, what happens to the **seven entries MC-038 §9b attributes to site furniture** — the five bottom near-misses `15_37_25`, `70`, `75`, `3187`, `13_33_41`, and `13_45_59` and `13_49_39` whose art runs into the taskbar — since that attribution is a prediction this story can falsify.
- **AC-6** — *Negative controls for a matcher's failure modes, each shown to fire or reported as not having fired.* Given any rule the document scores, when its controls section is read, then each of the following is run with its firing count out of the total. MC-034's and MC-038's four controls test a **pixel rule's** failure modes; a matcher's are different, so these are its own and at least (a), (b) and (c) must be **new inputs built for this story**. **(a) The same reader, changed** — the furniture present but not pixel-identical: the same template shifted vertically by a few rows, rendered at a different window height, or carrying different text in the nav bar. Firing means matching it anyway and placing an edge from a template that does not apply, or failing to match a reader that is plainly the same one; the document reports which of the two and at what perturbation the behaviour flips. **(b) Two different readers whose furniture is similar** — two templates that agree over a flat band; firing means attributing a screenshot to the wrong group. **(c) Artwork that repeats** — two screenshots sharing identical *artwork* rows rather than identical furniture (the same panel captured twice); firing means calling the artwork furniture and cropping it away, which is a clip and the worst defect in the ranking. **(d) The 7 `"expect": "flag"` tuning entries**, baseline **1 of 7 already cropped**; cropping more fires. Each control is shown firing on at least one parameterisation rather than passing vacuously; a control that fires on 0 of N is reported as **not having fired**, with a sentence on whether that is a result or a broken control.
- **AC-7** — *Every number is reproducible.* Given the document, when a number in it is questioned, then it names the command that produced it and the location of the harness, as MC-025's, MC-026's, MC-028's, MC-031's, MC-034's and MC-038's `## Notes` do. No number appears without one. Numbers read out of MC-026, MC-028, MC-031, MC-032, MC-034, MC-035, MC-038 or `corpus.md` cite the section they come from and are **not** re-measured. If it does not reproduce all four, nothing the harness says about a new family means anything and the document says so.

The document then states explicitly whether the rule it describes **is the thing the user declined in MC-031 Option B with one more strip removed**, since that is the question the 2026-09-17 decision makes of any furniture rule; a rule whose output still carries panel gutter above and below the panel is that thing, and saying so is the honest report. It ends with: a recommendation for **EPIC-07 story 4** (the row accuracy story) — whether a signal now exists that earns a bar, and what that bar would be; and a statement that **both of EPIC-07's conditions on learned or model-based detection are now discharged**, since the epic defers it *"until stories 2 and 3 have reported"* and this is story 3 — with a recommendation on whether to open it, the reason, and what it would need. If a rule *is* recommended, the document states what a held-out score would cost: which single **frozen** rule would be scored, that `Split::HeldOut` is selected **once**, that the unseen-*reader* number rests on five entries across three readers and is a direction rather than a percentage (`corpus.md`), and that **this story does not do it**. `docs/wiki/v2-candidates.md`'s table gains one row carrying the verdict, so the next agent opening v2 cold does not re-run this.

- **Settled — read out, never derive, tune or "calibrate".** The ten items

  band, its three entries and their exact numbers; MC-035's disjunction
  predicate; MC-019's 11 px window, zero-clip rule and 90 % bar; MC-005

  towards its target**. Do the same if it happens again.
- **Oracle-free, and the whole of the work.** What "furniture" *is*, and every
  threshold in it: how two rows are called identical, the tolerance, how
  agreement over a set of rows becomes a template, how templates are grouped
  and how a screenshot is attributed to one, the minimum run of agreeing rows
  that counts, and how a matched template's extent becomes a crop edge. The
  corpus is the oracle and a clip is the loss function. **Three controls have
  to be invented rather than inherited** — AC-6 (a), (b) and (c) — because
  MC-034's and MC-038's four all test failures a *pixel rule* had, and a
  matcher's failure modes are different in kind. So does AC-1's vacuity
  control, which is the only thing standing between this story and the
  discovery that flat rows resemble flat rows. The cautionary case is MC-019
  `## Notes` measurement 3: a statistic decisive at 78.6 % within 0 px,
  withdrawn when its control outscored it at 99.4 % on all *other* rows. A
  cross-image agreement statistic is squarely in that family.
- **Mechanical — pin exactly.** The denominator declaration and the 28-file
  list; AC-3's leave-one-out protocol statement; AC-5's reporting shape and
  wording; AC-7; AC-8's `v2-candidates.md` row.

**Success condition, which can come out either way.** This spike succeeds if a
reader can say which of these is true, with numbers: *a furniture-matching rule
exists that reaches MC-019's window on at least 20 of 21 marked tuning entries
with zero clips*; or *the furniture premise holds on G groups covering N of the
21 marked tuning entries at tolerance T, the perfect-furniture-oracle ceiling is
top T′ / bottom B′ / both C′ against MC-031's chrome oracle 0 of 21, the best
rule tried is M of 21 with K clips and a clip-free best of J, here is whether M
beats 8 of 21, and here is how much of MC-031's residual the site's own
furniture turned out to be*. It fails if it returns a score without AC-1's
premise measurement, a score without AC-2's ceiling, a score built on templates
that saw the entry they scored, or any number over a denominator other than 21
marked tuning entries.

**Model-choice success condition, which can also come out either way.** The one
verdict recorded in this repository (`story-authoring`, "Brief RED by oracle")
points at the **brief** rather than the model; MC-034's and MC-038's `## Notes`
both recorded the same conclusion on comparable spikes. So this story stays on
the default and spends its budget on the partition above. The conditions that
would overturn that: the investigation reports a score before the premise or
before the ceiling; it re-derives a settled ruling from an instrument; it
reports agreement without AC-1's vacuity control; it scores a template against
its own source image; or it reports any number over a denominator other than 21.
If instead AC-1 comes first, AC-2's ceiling second, the guard fires on a
template-construction call, and the controls are shown firing or honestly
reported as absent, the default was enough. **Record the verdict in `## Notes`
when the investigation ends** — a model choice with no recorded verdict is
folklore.

**Timebox, and it is the story's sizing.** Three stopping points, in order.

1. **Run AC-1 first.** If furniture is not pixel-identical or near-identical
   across screenshots in any discovered group, or if AC-1's vacuity control
   shows the agreement is flat rows agreeing with flat rows, that is the
   reasoned negative: write it, run no ceiling and no sweep, and stop. The
   epic's premise is a one-sentence assertion and this is the measurement of it.
2. **Then AC-2's ceiling.** If the perfect furniture oracle does not clear
   **MC-031's chrome oracle of 0 of 21** — or, more sharply, if its **bottom
   edge** does not clear MC-031's **2 of 21** and MC-038's reachable **13 of
   21** — that is the reasoned negative: write it, run no sweep, and stop.
   MC-038 §8b is the worked example of what to do when those two clauses
   disagree: run the sharper one, and record the reasoning in the document
   where a reader can disagree with it in one place. The bottom edge is the
   sharper clause because `panel-edge-search.md` 2c makes it the binding
   constraint.
3. **Then the sweep.** If the ceiling clears but two rule shapes have been
   scored without beating 8 of 21 and without improving on the clip-free 1 of
   21, that is also the reasoned negative.

As with MC-028, MC-031, MC-034, MC-035 and MC-038, **returning "no" early is
the valuable outcome** — and this story's "no" is worth more than any of
theirs, because it is what discharges EPIC-07's second condition on learned

## Out of scope

- **Scoring the held-out set, or building a template from a held-out entry.
  Not once, not "just to see".** No `Split::HeldOut` selection, no per-file
  held-out table read or printed, no aggregate held-out number, no threshold
  chosen, adjusted or rejected on anything a held-out entry produced, and — the
  route MC-038 did not have — **no furniture template constructed from, fitted
  to, or validated against a held-out image**. Baking a held-out screenshot
  into a rule contaminates it more thoroughly than scoring it would.
  `corpus.md`'s rule governs: an entry used for any such decision is **no
  longer held out whatever its `split` says**, and contamination runs one way.
  This is the single most important boundary in the story; AC-1's guard is how
  it is shown to have held, and AC-1 requires it shown firing on a
  template-construction call. The first held-out score belongs to whichever
  EPIC-07 story freezes a rule, and `corpus.md` requires that story to select
  `Split::HeldOut` deliberately, once, and say so in its own file.
- **A new denominator.** Every number is over the **28 tuning entries / 21
  marked**, which is what MC-019's, MC-026's, MC-028's, MC-031's, MC-032's,
  MC-034's, MC-035's and MC-038's numbers were measured over and the only
  reason this spike's numbers are comparable with theirs. If the investigation
  comes to believe another denominator is right, it **stops and asks the
  product owner**; it does not report one, and it does not report two.
- **Learned or model-based detection.** `EPIC-07` defers it *"until stories 2
  and 3 have reported"*, and the brief amendment of 2026-09-21 says explicitly
  that it *"stays deferred"* and that the amendment *"permits per-site
  furniture matching and nothing else"*. This **is** story 3, so AC-8 may
  **recommend** opening it and this story may not run one: no classifier, no
  embedding, no trained threshold, no segmentation model, no clustering that
  is a learned model in another name. Cross-image pixel agreement is the
  instrument; fitting a model to the corpus is not.
- **Colour and chroma**, ruled out in MC-025 `## Context` and not revisited by
  `EPIC-07`, by the brief amendment, or here. Furniture is matched on the luma
  plane the detector already sees (`codec::to_luma`).
- **Re-marking or re-tagging the corpus.** The marks stay **tight** by the
  user's decision of 2026-09-17, and MC-031's Option C was considered and
  declined; `EPIC-07` lists re-marking under "deliberately not in this epic".
  No re-tagging either, and this story has a specific temptation MC-038 did
  not: **it may not add a `site:` tag to any of the 28 tuning entries**, or
  otherwise attribute them to readers, even though grouping them by furniture
  is the whole instrument. `corpus.md` rules that attribution a person's call
  and that *"a guessed tag is worse than an absent one"*; `PRE_EPIC_07_SITES`

  third `diagonal-gutter` entry**, **no fourth gutter-crossing band entry**,
  **no sixth `w-network` attribution**, and no applying `overhang-text`, which
  `corpus.md` records as defined and deliberately unapplied. A discovered

  `manifest.json`.
