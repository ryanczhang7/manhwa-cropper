## Context

The user, 2026-09-23, on v1's output: *"I consider this a failure currently.
Not good enough for v1. The cropping from the side isnt tight enough and I
still see the page on the right and left side. And the app completely failed
to crop out the browser artifacts up top."* Measured the same day on a
release build of `cb2deef` over the 28 `tuning` entries: every marked crop
starts **97 to 306 px** above its mark and ends **16 to 310 px** below it, and
looked at on `Screenshot (67).png`, `Screenshot (3538).png` and
`2025-10-14 23_29_06.png`, the crop's top row is at y = 15..37 and keeps the
**browser tab strip, the bookmarks bar and the site navigation**, and its
bottom keeps the **Windows taskbar**. This story is the bug report for the
browser and OS half of that.

**The fix already exists as a measured instrument.** MC-031's chrome oracle
(`docs/wiki/chrome-row-search.md` §3b, §4, §9 Option B) locates the browser
viewport's first page row and the taskbar's first row from the share of
pixels **beside the page column, over the full image width**, that are flat
page background: browser chrome and taskbar are painted edge to edge and have
no flat margin, reader page rows do. On the 21 marked tuning entries it never
clips, lies outside the mark on **19 of 19** entries where it speaks, and
declines on the two `2025-08-05` WebPs (§5e). The user's decision of
2026-09-23 is to **ship it now, as an internal stage of the crop**.

**What this story is, and what it is not — read both.**

- It is **progress toward `EPIC-07` story 4** — *the crop removes browser, OS
  and reader furniture, and nothing below it* — whose bar is containment of the
  mark plus absence of furniture (`product-brief.md` §5, third amendment of
  2026-09-21). This story delivers the browser and OS two-thirds of
  "absence" and holds containment absolute.
- It is **not** the bar, and it must not be reported as meeting it. MC-031's
  Option B — chrome removal *as the product's criterion* — was declined by the
  user on 2026-09-17 because the site's own navigation survived; `EPIC-07`
  permits it only *"as an internal stage of a rule that goes further"*. That is
  exactly what this is. The rule that goes further is
  [MC-050](MC-050.md).
- **After this story, v1 still lacks:** (1) removal of the **reader's own
  header, navigation and footer** — MC-050, then a feature if MC-050 finds a
  rule; (2) the **side strip** of page background — [MC-049](MC-049.md),
  `EPIC-08`; (3) chrome removal on any screenshot where the page background
  beside the column is not flat enough for the oracle to speak (the two WebPs
  in the tuning set, and whatever held-out entries share that property);
  (4) a **held-out** score of the full containment-and-absence predicate,
  which cannot exist until (1) does.

The required gate that fails if this story's artifact breaks: `unit`, through
AC-6 and AC-7's synthetic fixtures. The corpus half (AC-1 to AC-5, AC-8) is
seen only by `integration`, which is optional — hence `required_gates`.

## Acceptance criteria

The **viewport rows** below are MC-031's located values, read out of
`chrome-row-search.md` §4 per file: the first page row (`chromeEnd`) is 133,
137 or 167, and the first taskbar row is 1392 or 1400. "The crop's rows"
means `y .. y + h - 1` of the rect `process_file` returns.

- **AC-1** — *The bug, reproduced.* Given the 19 marked `tuning` entries for
  which §4 lists a `chromeEnd` and a `taskbar`, when `process_file` runs at
  `Tuning::default()` on `main` at `cb2deef`, then the crop's first row lies
  **above** that entry's `chromeEnd` and its last row lies **at or below** its
  `taskbar` row — on **19 of 19** (RED confirms the count; §5f already records
  the shipped bottom at 1439, inside the taskbar). This is the failing test
  that becomes the regression test: after the fix it asserts the opposite, on
  19 of 19.
- **AC-2** — *Browser chrome and taskbar are gone where the oracle speaks.*
  Given the same 19 entries, when `process_file` runs at `Tuning::default()`,
  then every crop row `r` satisfies `chromeEnd <= r < taskbar` for that entry.
  *Control on the number:* the same predicate over today's crops fails on 19
  of 19 (AC-1). *Control on the metric:* the predicate with each entry's
  `chromeEnd` replaced by 0 and `taskbar` by the image height must pass on
  today's crops — so the check is reading the viewport rows and not something
  else.
- **AC-3** — *The two WebPs, a named limitation.* Given
  `2025-08-05 00_11_13.webp` and `2025-08-05 00_11_27.webp`, where the oracle
  declines at its full-margin setting, when `process_file` runs, then each
  crop is **identical** (`x`, `y`, `w`, `h`) to the one `cb2deef` produces —
  the stage declines and moves nothing — and neither clips its mark. Chrome
  removal on these two is a known limitation of this story (Context, "v1 still
  lacks" item 3). *Amended in RED, 2026-09-23 — see `## Amendments`.*
- **AC-4** — *Containment is untouched.* Given all 21 marked `tuning` entries,
  when `process_file` runs, then every `Cropped` rect contains the marked rect
  entirely — **0 clips**, as today. The existing
  `no_crop_clips_a_marked_page` and its inset-10 control stay as they are.
- **AC-5** — *Flags do not move.* Given the seven `"expect": "flag"` tuning
  entries, when `process_file` runs, then the six MC-026 AC-4 pins are still
  `Flagged` for `Detector(NoBorderFound)`, and `2025-02-27 22_46_15.png` —
  cropped today, the known baseline MC-026 and MC-019 name — is reported in
  the handoff with its before and after rect and is not pinned either way.
- **AC-6** — *The stage, on synthetic images (required `unit` gate).* Given a
  generated screenshot — a textured page column on a flat page background,
  with a full-width textured band across the top (tab strip) and another
  across the bottom (taskbar) — when `detect` runs, then the crop contains the
  page art and **no row of either band**. Given the same image with the bands
  painted as flat page background instead, the crop is identical to the one
  the pipeline produced before this story (the stage moves nothing when there
  is nothing to remove). Given an image whose page background beside the
  column varies row to row so that no viewport can be located, the stage
  declines and the crop is identical to the one the pipeline produced before
  this story.
- **AC-7** — *The margin does not put chrome back.* Given a generated
  screenshot whose page art starts on the viewport's first page row, when
  `detect` runs at `margin_px` = 3, then the crop's first row is that viewport
  row, not three rows above it inside the chrome; and the same for the last
  row and the taskbar. *Control:* at `margin_px` = 0 the rows are the same.
- **AC-8** — *The columns do not move.* Given all 21 marked `tuning` entries,
  when `process_file` runs, then each crop's `x` and `w` are **exactly** what
  they are on `cb2deef` (pinned per file from the RED measurement). The side
  edges are [MC-049](MC-049.md)'s, and this story is not allowed to move them
  as a side effect.
- **AC-9** — *The record.* Given the story is done, then `architecture.md`'s
  `cropper-core` pipeline lists the viewport stage in its place, decision 14
  carries a dated paragraph saying browser chrome and the taskbar are removed
  as an internal stage and **that the `EPIC-07` bar is not yet met**, and this
  story's `## Notes` carries the before/after row numbers for all 21 entries.

## Model guidance

Model: default. The recorded verdict (`story-authoring`, "Brief RED by
oracle") points at the brief, not the model, and this story's hard part is
porting a measured instrument faithfully, which a stronger model does not buy.

**Oracle partition.**

- **Settled — read out, never re-derived or calibrated.** The oracle's
  parameterisation as MC-031 scored it: statistic *Bg* (share of margin pixels
  within `uniform_tolerance` of the image's modal page-background tone, the
  tone taken once per image as the modal per-row margin median in 8-level
  bins), selector *ChromeStrip* (top = end of the leading non-page-like run,
  bottom = start of the trailing one), threshold **0.90**, minimum run
  **16** rows, gap 0, **full** margin width (`chrome-row-search.md` §3b and
  `ceiling.rs` line 146 of the MC-031 harness). The per-file `chromeEnd` /
  `taskbar` values in §4. §3c's "rows 0..100 are chrome on all 21". MC-028
  §5a's 115 for the WebP. `uniform_tolerance` 10, `margin_px` 3, the six flag
  pins, the containment predicate.
- **Evidence, not settled — verify, do not assume.** "Never clips" and "outside
  the mark on 19 of 19" are MC-031's measurements through **its own harness**,
  which is spike code outside the repository
  (`...ec112bbb-.../scratchpad/mc031`). RED re-measures both against the
  committed implementation's output. The harness may be **read for the
  algorithm**; its code is not reviewed and is **not copied** — the stage is
  re-derived in `crates/core` with tests.
- **Oracle-free — invent, with a control that fires.** AC-6's synthetic
  screenshot and the decline condition: what "cannot locate a viewport" means
  on a generated image, and a fixture on each side of it.
- **Mechanical — pin exactly.** AC-1's 19-of-19 count, AC-5's six names, AC-8's
  21 `(x, w)` pairs, AC-7's rows.

**Success condition, which can come out either way.** RED's re-measurement of
the committed stage reproduces §4's located rows on at least 18 of the 19
entries to the row. If it does not, the port is wrong or §4 is, and RED stops
and says which before any GREEN — it does not "calibrate" the threshold toward
§4. **Record the verdict in `## Notes`.**

### The export shape the tests pin - fact, not suggestion

```rust
// crates/core/src/lib.rs
pub mod viewport;

// crates/core/src/viewport.rs
#[derive(Debug, PartialEq /* Eq, Clone, Copy: your choice */)]
pub struct Viewport {
    pub top: u32,    // first row of the browser viewport (chromeEnd)
    pub bottom: u32, // first row of the taskbar - exclusive
}

pub fn locate(img: &Luma, column: Rect, t: &Tuning) -> Option<Viewport>;
```

- `Viewport` is **built by struct literal** in the tests
  (`Viewport { top: 40, bottom: 200 }`), so it has exactly these two public
  fields and no others. `Debug` and `PartialEq` are required (`assert_eq!` on
  `Option<Viewport>`).
- `column` is the rect `page_column` returns inside `detect` - **before**
  `margin::expand`. The tests compose it from the existing public stages
  themselves; no helper is pinned.
- `locate` is **the full-margin oracle as settled**: Bg, ChromeStrip, 0.90,
  min run 16, gap 0, the full image width outside `column`, `uniform_tolerance`
  from `t`. `corpus_viewport_stage` requires it to return `None` on both WebPs,
  and AC-3 requires `detect` then to produce exactly today's crop there: the
  stage declines and nothing else moves.
- It must return `None` when there is no pixel beside `column`.

Not constrained: where in `detect` the stage runs, whether it narrows the rect
before or after the margin (only the resulting rows are pinned), whether
threshold and min run are constants or new `Tuning` fields (the tests never
build `Tuning` by full literal), what `locate` returns when every row is
page-like (`Some(0..h)` or `None` - the blank-band identity passes either
way), how `Detection::trimmed` treats a viewport cut (the synthetic scenes are
already `trimmed: true`).
