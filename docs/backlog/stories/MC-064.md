## Context

**The request (2026-09-30):** the user's ruling on MC-063's clips was
*"Write up and file"*. This is the first of the two stories filed.

fresh screenshots, at `1438b2b`. The crates there are identical to
`d2876f5`. The result was **3 clips of 25, and 21 of 25 meeting the bar
against 23**
([`held-out-score.md`](../../wiki/held-out-score.md), first section). Under

Having been read per file, the four are `tuning` for any later rule
(`corpus.md`, "The fresh set is spent: MC-063"). The fix for them,
[MC-065](MC-065.md), is fitted while looking at them. A held-out entry that a
rule is designed against cannot stay `held-out`. The precedents are MC-052,
MC-053 and, closest, [MC-056](MC-056.md), which moved MC-051's three read
entries. MC-063 was a spike and could not write `fixtures/`, so the move is
this chore.

**What the move is.** It changes four `"split"` values in
`fixtures/corpus/manifest.json` from `held-out` to `tuning`. No `expect` and
no tag changes. No production code changes.

**What it is not.** The other 21 fresh entries stay `held-out` in the
manifest. They are spent (any later run over them is a re-score), but they
were not read per file. This story does not move them. See Out of scope.

**The counts after the move:**

|---|---|---|

| held-out (marked + flag) | 25 (25 + 0) | **21 (21 + 0)** |

  - `MIN_HELD_OUT_FLAGS` and `MIN_UNSEEN_SITES` are pinned equal to 0, and

measures them, as MC-056 did. RED copies `HEAD` into a scratch directory

- `cargo test --workspace --release --no-fail-fast -- --ignored` (the
  `integration` command plus `--no-fail-fast`, because without it the gate
  stops at the first failing binary; MC-056 `## Context`).

The measured list replaces this one in `## Notes` before any pin is written.
Those runs touch only `tuning` entries: every suite filters on
`Split::Tuning`, and the 21 unread fresh entries stay `held-out`.

### How the clips enter `tuning`

This is Open question 1. **Recommended:** name them as known exceptions,
**exact in both directions**. The precedents are:
- `KNOWN_AMBIGUOUS` (MC-056);
- `KNOWN_CROPPED_FLAGS` and `KNOWN_MISSES` (MC-062).

Each list pins **today's crop exactly**, rect and outcome at both margins. A
test naming the list then fails in two cases:
- any *other* marked `tuning` entry clips;
- a listed entry stops clipping, or its crop moves at all.

So MC-065 has to empty the list, and cannot leave a stale exception behind.
`f13`, which does not clip, is named the same way in whichever suites its
wide crop fails.

### The route: RED, then SCAFFOLD

`fixtures/corpus/manifest.json` and every file under `crates/engine/tests/`
classify as `test`, and GREEN may not write `test`. The route is MC-042's and
MC-056's:
- **RED** writes the new pins and exception lists, and watches them fail
  against the unmoved manifest.
- **SCAFFOLD** moves the four and writes `corpus.md`.

No production source is written. `## Scaffold inventory` names the manifest
and the test files, as MC-056's does.

**Required gate that would fail if the artifact broke:**
- `unit` (`corpus_manifest.rs`) for AC-1 and AC-2;
- `integration` for AC-3 and AC-4, hence `required_gates: [integration]`.

- any pin row is typed rather than measured;
- any of the 21 unread fresh entries is decoded by a test.

## Open questions — answer before it starts

1. **Three of the four screenshots are ones where the app cut into the art.
   Once they join the practice set, the practice checks will fail on them
   until the fix lands. How should that be handled?**
   - *Recommended: list them by name as known problems*, the way the
     ambiguous page (MC-056) and the two cropped "leave alone" images
     (MC-062) are listed today. The list records exactly what the app does to
     each one now. It fails if a fourth screenshot starts being cut, and also
     if one of these stops being cut, so the fix has to clear the list and
     cannot forget to.
   - *Alternative:* do the move and the fix in one story. But the fix cannot
     be tested on these screenshots until they are practice, and one story
     doing both is two cycles.
2. **Should the other 21 new screenshots also move to practice now?** They
   have been tested once and passed, and nobody has looked at them one by
   one.
   - *Recommended: no, leave them where they are.* They cannot give another
     real test score, so they are spent. They are still unexamined, though,
     and that may be useful to know later. Moving them is cheap at any time.
   - *Alternative:* move all 25, as MC-062 moved the old 23. The practice
     set gains 21 more screenshots to check changes against.

## Out of scope

- **Any change to the crop or the detector.** That is [MC-065](MC-065.md).
- **Moving the other 21 fresh entries**, unless Open question 2 is answered
  "move all". They stay `held-out` and spent.
- **Any mark change.** Nobody has checked the four marks against the images,
  and this story does not. If RED's measurements suggest a mark error (as
  MC-056's Open question 3 did), it is put to the user, not corrected here.
- **Viewing the four images.** Nothing in this story needs a look at the
  pixels. The diagnosis is MC-065's, under its own open question.
- **Any change to `held-out-score.md`'s numbers**, or any re-run of the
  fresh set. MC-063's score stands as recorded.
- **Changing the `integration` gate's command** (`--no-fail-fast`), as in
  MC-056.
- **A new held-out draw.** That is its own MC-062-shaped story.
