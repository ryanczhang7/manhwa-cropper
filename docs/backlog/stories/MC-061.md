detection "the only untried signal class left for the general rule" and makes
opening it a separate decision with its own cost. Three reasoned negatives
since then:

- MC-038 closed 2D region structure;
- MC-050 found no rule needed on its tuning set, but scored none;
- MC-059 ([`site-header-search.md`](../../wiki/site-header-search.md)) found
  no reader-agnostic same-tone-header rule. Its §8 names learned detection as
  one of three options.

Per-site matching, the other one, stays parked as [MC-041](MC-041.md).

**What it would cost, from the brief:** it needs the grown corpus most of all
(tens of marked screenshots are far short of what a model trains on), and
under the offline constraint any model ships inside the exe and runs locally.

## Before it can start

2. A corpus large enough to train and to hold out, which today's is not. The

3. A fresh held-out score shows a gap that the hand-written rules do not

## Acceptance criteria (outline only, not frozen)

- What model class fits inside the exe and runs offline within the brief's
  time budget.
- How many marked screenshots it needs, measured by a learning curve on the
  tuning set, never on held-out.
- Whether it beats today's crop on a leave-one-reader-out check, the test
  MC-059 used.
