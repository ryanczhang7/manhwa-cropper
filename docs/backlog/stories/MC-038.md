## Context

[`EPIC-07`](../epics/EPIC-07.md) `## Stories` item 2, written out. The epic's
words are *"does 2D structure locate the panel edge where 1D projections
cannot?"* and its claim is structural rather than incremental:

> MC-025, MC-028, MC-031, MC-032, MC-034 and MC-035 each reduced the image to a
> **1D row statistic** [...] A speech bubble is a *small 2D object*; projected
> onto a row it contaminates the whole row, so a gutter containing one arc reads
> as content. [...] **no row statistic can work when the thing that
> distinguishes gutter from art is spatial extent**.

`crates/core/src/` is `content.rs`, `decide.rs`, `edges.rs`, `flat.rs`,
`margin.rs` and `trim.rs` — there is no connected-component, contour or region
analysis anywhere in the tree, so the claim has never been tested. This spike
tests it. **The deliverable is a document, `docs/wiki/region-row-search.md`,
plus one row in `docs/wiki/v2-candidates.md`'s "do not re-run these" table.**
Not a shipped rule, not a `Tuning` field, not a threshold.

Three findings from those documents shape this story more than the scores do:

1. **The whole deficit is the bottom edge.** `panel-edge-search.md` section 2c
   measures reachability per edge: **top 20 of 21, bottom 11 of 21, both 10 of
   21**. Ten of the eleven unreachable entries fail on the bottom alone. A 2D
   family that improves the top edge improves nothing that matters.
2. **MC-034 already took the first step away from flatness and it was not
   enough.** Its coverage family asks *how much of the row's width is not
   background* rather than *is this row flat* — `v2-candidates.md` calls that
   "the right question", and it still reaches 8 of 21 with 9 clips. So "not
   flatness" is not the same as "not a row statistic": `cover(y)` is still one
   scalar per row. **This story's whole novelty is the part `cover(y)` throws
   away — vertical extent and connectivity** — and AC-3 exists to force that
   claim to be demonstrated rather than asserted.
3. **The trade-off, not the headline, is the thing to break.**
   `v2-candidates.md`: *"the rules that rarely clip are the ones that overshoot
   so far they place nothing, and the rules that place anything clip
   repeatedly. There is no middle, and no band width creates one. A v2 signal
   has to break that trade-off, not move along it."* AC-4 requires the document
   to say which of those two it did.

The bottom-edge failures MC-034 records come in exactly two shapes, and both are
worth stating here because a 2D method has a different relationship to each:
**the page's art runs continuously into the taskbar** (`13_45_59` art 364…1440,
`13_49_39` art 331…1440 — no gutter to stop at, but the taskbar is a *region*),
and **the mark lies past an internal gutter the scan stops at** (`2708`,
`3538`, `2698` — which is the gutter-crossing band, settled below).

### The gate question

The required gate that fails if this story's artifact breaks is, taken
literally, **none** — the artifact is a markdown document and no gate reads it.
Recording that and stopping would repeat the mistake `story-authoring` and
MC-037's `## Notes` both warn about from opposite directions, so the question
is asked one level down: *what in this repository is every number in the
document denominated by, and what checks it?*

The answer is the `Split::Tuning` filter MC-037 added, and the gate that
exercises it is **`integration`, which is `optional` and `slow`**:

```
$ bash scripts/gates.sh --list
unit         required  .      cargo test --workspace
                              floor:    47
integration  optional  .      cargo test --workspace --release -- --ignored
                              evidence: test result: ok\. [1-9][0-9]*
                              slow:     release rebuild of the workspace plus the 100-image
                                        timing run and the corpus; minutes (left out of --fast)
```

`corpus_manifest.rs` runs in the **required `unit`** gate and is what would fail
if this story touched `fixtures/corpus/manifest.json`, `docs/wiki/corpus.md`'s
parsed tables, or the 60 MiB byte ceiling — all of which `## Out of scope`
forbids. But `corpus_manifest.rs` only checks the corpus is *well formed*. The
two suites that actually **score** it, and that carry `corpus.rs::tuning_only()`
and the filter inside `corpus_accuracy.rs::run_corpus()`, run only under
`integration`. If that filter is removed or narrowed, every baseline in the
table above silently changes denominator from 21 marked tuning entries to 24
more it was never measured over — and `All required gates passed` prints.

That exact sentence is not hypothetical. MC-037's `## Notes`:

> `integration` is optional, so `All required gates passed` printed over it.
> That combination — an optional gate carrying the only check that touches the
> thing a story built — is the failure mode this story's own `## Context`
> describes for `required_gates`, showing up from the other direction.

So **`required_gates: [integration]`**, decided here at PLANNED and not left for
GATES. Two things about that escalation are worth knowing before it is relied
on, because both were checked:

- `scripts/gates.sh` binds a story's `required_gates` while it runs, and
  `scripts/check-boundaries.sh` re-checks them against the recorded run — but
  only inside the `## Gate results` block, and line 196 exempts a spike from
  having one at all (`REVIEW:spike|DONE:spike) note "spike story; gate record
  not required"`). **For a spike, therefore, the escalation did nothing unless
  a full gate run happened to be made anyway.**

  This story is where that was found, and it is a hole in the harness rather
  than a problem with this story, so it is fixed at the source:
  **[MC-039](MC-039.md)** narrows the exemption to "no gate record required
  *unless* `required_gates` is non-empty", and is a `depends_on` of this story.
  An earlier draft carried a criterion (AC-8) demanding a full recorded run, to
  work around the hole from inside the story. That was declined at PLANNED on
  2026-09-19: it makes a commit-integrity check into a story's acceptance
  criterion, which no prior spike has, and the next spike author would have
  copied it without knowing why. Fixing the exemption enforces the escalation
  for every future spike instead of this one.
- The `integration` gate has an `evidence` line and **no `floor`**, so a suite
  that shrank would still satisfy it — 14 tests becoming 1 passes. Filed as
  **[MC-040](MC-040.md)** and deliberately not fixed here: a `floor` needs a
  gate probe of its own, and the first `test result: ok. N` line under
  `--ignored` is test-binary-order dependent, which is the trap
  `project.conf` already documents at length for the `unit` floor. It does not
  block this story.

### The denominator, and the one rule this story must not break

**Every number in the document is measured over the 28 `tuning` entries — 21
marked and 7 `"expect": "flag"`.** Verified against
`fixtures/corpus/manifest.json` on 2026-09-19: 59 entries, **28 `tuning` / 31
`held-out`**; 14 flag entries, **7 in each split**; so held-out is 24 marked and
7 flag, across 7 readers (`toongod` 10, `rolia-scans` 8, `w-network` 5,
`xbato` 3, `manhwaclan` 2, `kunmanga` 2, `demonicrevolution` 1).

**The held-out set is not scored here. Not once, not "just to see".** A spike is
tuning work by definition — it fits an approach and reads per-file results — so
it runs over `Split::Tuning` only. `docs/wiki/corpus.md`, "The rule, stated so
it cannot be read two ways":

> no threshold is chosen, adjusted or rejected on the strength of a held-out
> result; no per-file table of held-out results is read while a rule is still
> being changed; and an entry that has been used to make any such decision is no
> longer held out, whatever its `split` says. [...] contamination only runs one
> way.

`corpus.rs::tuning_only()` and `corpus_accuracy.rs::run_corpus()` enforce this
in the repository. **This story's harness lives outside the repository and can
import neither**, which is precisely why AC-1 asks for a guard rather than a
filter: a filter that is merely applied looks identical, from the document, to
one that was forgotten.

The second reason the denominator is fixed is comparability. `corpus.md`: the
filter "keeps **MC-019's, MC-026's and MC-032's recorded numbers meaning what
they meant** [...] without the filter their denominator would silently have
become fifty-nine". A new denominator is a product decision — if this spike
wants one it stops and asks; `## Out of scope` says so.

### What is settled, and is read out rather than re-derived

Three rulings bear directly on how a row edge is scored. All three are the
user's, all three are recorded on `docs/wiki/corpus.md`, and none is
re-derivable by an agent.

1. **The marking rule, of 2026-09-17**, which supersedes MC-018's wider
   phrasing: *"the tightest axis-aligned rectangle containing all of one page's
   own artwork. A panel's own overhang extends its rectangle; a neighbouring
   panel's overhang, or a caption box sitting in the gutter, does not."* The
   named cases are `2744` bottom (caption box outside) and `2630` top (the
   neighbour's bubble outside).
2. **The diagonal-gutter tolerance — two entries**, `Screenshot (93).jpg` (top)
   and `2025-10-20 15_37_25.png` (bottom): a mark on the higher edge, the lower
   edge or the middle of the diagonal is equally acceptable. MC-019 still scores
   them exactly, and `corpus.md` forbids adding a third on an agent's judgement.
3. **The gutter-crossing tolerance — three entries**, and these numbers are read
   out, not measured:

   | Entry | top of the gutter | recorded mark | the band |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `Screenshot (2708).jpg` | 1305 | 1322 | 17 px |
   | `Screenshot (3538).png` | 1314 | 1330 | 16 px |
   | `Screenshot (2698).jpg` | 1080 | 1085 | 5 px |

   MC-035 formalised the predicate as a **disjunction of two exact MC-019
   scorings** — a hit against either expected bottom, a clip only against both —
   and explicitly *not* the contiguous widening. A fourth banded entry is a
   person's call, on the same footing as a third diagonal.

The seven entries the epic names as deciding this spike — `2708`, `2630`,
`1661`, `13_33_41`, `23_30_20`, `13_45_59`, `70` — are MC-032's **cause 1**,
"a neighbouring bubble crosses the gutter" (`panel-edge-search.md` 2a). All
seven were checked in the manifest on 2026-09-19: all present, all marked, all
`split: tuning`.

## Acceptance criteria

The deliverable is one document, `docs/wiki/region-row-search.md`, and one row
in `docs/wiki/v2-candidates.md`'s "Already measured — do not re-run these"
table. These criteria are about them.

- **AC-1** — *The denominator is declared before any score, and the held-out set is shown to have been guarded rather than merely filtered.* Given the document, when its first evidence section is read, then it states that every number in it is measured over the **28 `tuning`** entries (**21 marked**, 7 `"expect": "flag"`), names that count, lists the 28 files by name, and states that none of the 31 `held-out` entries appears in the list; and it names the mechanism in its own harness that enforces this, given that the harness cannot import `corpus.rs::tuning_only()`. *Control, which decides whether any of the above is decorative:* the harness must **refuse to run** when handed a `held-out` entry, and the document shows that refusal firing — the entry's name, the harness's own error, and the fact that **nothing was scored and no per-file result was produced**. The refusal must occur before any scoring, so that demonstrating it cannot itself contaminate. A document that reports "28" without showing the guard fire has shown that a filter was applied on one run, not that it is enforced; and a harness that has a filter and no guard is reported as having none.
- **AC-2** — *The per-edge ceiling for the region family is measured before any rule is ranked, and stated against MC-034's.* Given the document, when its ceiling section is read, then for each of the **21 marked tuning entries and both of its edges (42 edges)** it reports whether **any** parameterisation of the region family places that edge inside MC-019's window — the expected rect expanded by `margin_px + 8` = 11 px — without clipping, in the per-file shape of `panel-edge-search.md` section 2c, with the three totals: **top reachable, bottom reachable, and both (the ceiling)**. Each total is stated beside MC-034's measured **20 of 21 / 11 of 21 / 10 of 21**, and the entries the region family reaches that MC-034's two families did not are **named individually**, as are the entries it still does not reach. *Controls on the number:* a ceiling of 21 of 21 is the vacuous answer and is rejected — if no entry is excluded the document must say what it believes MC-034's measurement got wrong, with the images; and a ceiling that lands at 10 of 21 on the **same eleven entries** MC-034 names (`23_30_20`, `15_37_25`, `13_33_41`, `13_45_59`, `13_49_39`, `70`, `75`, `93`, `103`, `2744`, `3187`) is reported as a **reproduction of MC-034's ceiling by a different instrument** — which closes the epic's premise and is a valid and valuable finding, not a failure to deliver.
- **AC-3** — *The family is shown to use a property no per-row scalar carries, on at least one of the seven bubble entries, with both numbers.* Given the document, when its family section is read, then it states in one sentence the **2D property** each rule decides on — a component's bounding box, its area, its width relative to the page column `flat::page_column` finds, its vertical extent, or another named property — and states why that property **cannot be recovered from any single scalar per row**. It then demonstrates the claim on at least one named entry from `2708`, `2630`, `1661`, `13_33_41`, `23_30_20`, `13_45_59`, `70`: at the marked edge and at a row the overhanging bubble occupies, it reports **MC-034's `cover(y)`** and its own 2D property, and shows that `cover(y)` does not separate the two rows while the 2D property does — four numbers and the rows they were taken at. *Control, and the one that decides the epic's premise:* if the 2D property separates no better than `cover(y)` on any of the seven, the family is reported as **MC-034's coverage family under a new name**, in those words, and the document says that EPIC-07's structural claim about 1D projections is not supported by measurement. Either outcome is a result; a family asserted to be 2D-shaped without this demonstration is not.
- **AC-4** — *Both of MC-019's numbers, per entry, plus the clip-free best and the position on the trade-off.* Given the document, when its scoring section is read, then for every rule it scores it reports **how many of the 21 marked tuning entries land inside the 11 px window** and **how many are clipped**, computed over the **final, margin-expanded, clamped** rect as MC-028 section 5b, MC-031 section 8 and MC-034 section 6 do, with a **per-entry table**; and it reports the **best clip-free** member separately, because MC-005 decision 13 ranks a clip above every other defect. The result is stated against the baselines in `## Context` in these words: **"beats 8 of 21" or "does not beat 8 of 21"**, and **"the clip-free best is N of 21"** beside MC-028's 1 of 21 and MC-034/MC-035's 0 of 21 (and MC-035's stronger form, that no member of either prior family is clip-free at all). Finally it answers `v2-candidates.md`'s question explicitly: whether the family **breaks** the clips-versus-placements trade-off or only **moves along** it, with the evidence — a member that both places more and clips less than every prior member breaks it; anything else does not. The two `diagonal-gutter` entries are scored **exactly as MC-019 scores them**; the three gutter-crossing entries may additionally be reported under MC-035's disjunction predicate **alongside** the headline and must never be folded into it.
- **AC-5** — *Four negative controls, each shown to fire or reported as not having fired.* Given any rule the document scores, when its controls section is read, then each of the following is run against every parameterisation, with its firing count out of the total: **(a) a neighbour's overhang** — MC-034 section 4(a)'s synthetic page, panel at rows 200…800 with a narrow caption box (80 of 400 columns) in the gutter at 820…880 and the right bottom edge at **800**; firing means over-reaching to 880, and the rate is reported beside MC-034's measured **45 of 540 (8.3 %)** for coverage and **60 of 240 (25 %)** for spread, since a lower rate is the strongest evidence the 2D property does the job the marking rule asks and a higher one is evidence against it. **(b) a flat full-width row inside the panel** — MC-026 finding 5's defect: a page at 200…1000 with a flat full-width band at 600…640, right answer 200…1000; MC-034 reports this firing on **540 of 540** for structural reasons (the band is 40 rows and its largest `gap` was 24), and a region family's claim to be different is directly testable here, so the count is reported whichever way it falls. **(c) the 7 `"expect": "flag"` tuning entries**, whose no-regression baseline is **1 of 7 already cropped** by the shipped pipeline (`2025-02-27 22_46_15.png`); cropping more than 1 of 7 is a regression and fires. **(d) a control on the 2D property this story invents** — an input whose *page's own* art is genuinely narrow or genuinely broken into pieces, so that a rule keyed to a large wide component under-reaches or discards the page; the document names the input, the right answer, and what the family scores on it. A metric whose only control tests the case it was designed for is untested, and (a) to (c) are all inherited. Each control is shown firing on at least one parameterisation rather than passing vacuously; a control that fires on 0 of N is reported as **not having fired**, with a sentence on whether that is a result or a broken control.
- **AC-6** — *Every number is reproducible.* Given the document, when a number in it is questioned, then it names the command that produced it and the location of the harness, as MC-025's, MC-026's, MC-028's, MC-031's and MC-034's `## Notes` do. No number appears without one. Numbers read out of MC-026, MC-028, MC-031, MC-032, MC-034, MC-035 or `corpus.md` cite the section they come from and are **not** re-measured.
- **AC-7** — *The verdict, and what EPIC-07 stories 3 and 4 inherit.* Given the investigation has ended, when the document's first section is read, then it states one of exactly two verdicts: **a recommended rule**, with its score and clip count under MC-019's bar of 20 of 21 with zero clips; or **a reasoned negative** — no rule tried reaches the bar — with the shapes tried and why each failed. Either verdict is stated against **both** yardsticks: AC-2's ceiling and AC-4's baselines. The document then ends with a recommendation for **EPIC-07 story 3** (known site furniture) — run it, do not run it, or run it in a changed form — with the reason, since the epic makes story 3 conditional on this one; and, if a rule is recommended, it states what a held-out score would cost: which single **frozen** rule would be scored, that `Split::HeldOut` is selected **once**, that the unseen-*reader* number rests on five entries across three readers and is a direction rather than a percentage (`corpus.md`), and that **this story does not do it**. A reasoned negative is a valid and expected outcome, as it was for MC-028, MC-031, MC-034 and MC-035; the value of this spike is highest when it returns "no" early. `docs/wiki/v2-candidates.md`'s table gains one row carrying the verdict, so the next agent opening v2 cold does not re-run this.

## Model guidance

Model: default. Oracle partition, in the form `story-authoring` "Brief RED by
oracle" asks for.

- **Settled — read out, never re-derived.** The marking rule and its two named
  cases (`2744` bottom, `2630` top); the diagonal-gutter tolerance and its two
  entries; the gutter-crossing band and its three entries with their exact
  numbers; MC-035's disjunction predicate; MC-019's 11 px window
  (`margin_px + 8`, `margin_px` = 3), its zero-clip rule and the 90 % bar;
  MC-005 decision 13; MC-027's columns, taken as they are; the split
  assignment, the contamination rule and `PRE_EPIC_07_SITES`; MC-032's four
  causes and the entries each names; every baseline in `## Context`. These came
  from a person looking at images or from a user's decision, and
  `panel-edge-search.md` 2d is the standing warning about what happens when an
  agent re-derives one: MC-032's flatness instrument, re-implemented from its
  own description, gave 14 of 21 where MC-032 recorded 16, and was abandoned
  rather than calibrated towards its target. Do the same if it happens again.
- **Oracle-free, and the whole of the work.** The region construction and every
  threshold in it: how a pixel is called background, what connectivity means,
  the minimum component size, how a component is attributed to the page rather
  than to a neighbour, and how the page's rows are read off the components that
  survive. The corpus is the oracle and a clip is the loss function. Two
  controls have to be **invented** rather than inherited: AC-3's separation
  demonstration, which is the only thing standing between this story and a
  rediscovery of `cover(y)`; and AC-5(d), because (a), (b) and (c) all come from
  MC-034 and all test failures the *previous* family had. The cautionary case is
  MC-019 `## Notes` measurement 3 — a statistic that looked decisive at 78.6 %
  within 0 px and was withdrawn when its control outscored it at 99.4 % on all
  *other* rows. A 2D-region metric is in exactly that family.
- **Mechanical.** AC-1's file list, AC-4's reporting shape, AC-6, AC-7's
  `v2-candidates.md` row.

**Success condition, which can come out either way.** This spike succeeds if a
reader can say which of these is true, with numbers: *a region-structure rule
exists that reaches MC-019's window on at least 20 of 21 marked tuning entries
with zero clips*; or *the per-edge ceiling is top T / bottom B / both C, the
best rule tried is M of 21 with K clips and a clip-free best of J, here is
whether M beats 8 of 21, and here is whether the 2D property separates a bubble
row from a marked edge where `cover(y)` does not*. It fails if it returns a
score without the ceiling, a family without AC-3's demonstration, or a
denominator other than 21 marked tuning entries.

**Model-choice success condition, which can also come out either way.** The one
verdict recorded in this repository (`story-authoring`, "Brief RED by oracle")
points at the **brief** rather than the model, and MC-034's `## Notes` recorded
the same conclusion on a comparable spike, so this story stays on the default
and spends its budget on the partition above. The conditions that would overturn

AC-3's four numbers; or it reports any number over a denominator other than 21.
If instead the ceiling comes first, the guard in AC-1 fires, and AC-3's
separation is demonstrated or honestly reported as absent, the default was
enough. **Record the verdict in `## Notes` when the investigation ends** — a
model choice with no recorded verdict is folklore.

**Timebox, and it is the story's sizing.** Run AC-2's ceiling first. If it does
not clear MC-034's **both-edges 10 of 21** — or, more sharply, if the **bottom
edge** does not clear MC-034's 11 of 21 — that is the reasoned negative: write
it, do not run a ranked sweep, and stop. If the ceiling clears but two rule
shapes have been scored without beating 8 of 21 and without improving on the
clip-free 1 of 21, that is also the reasoned negative. As with MC-028, MC-031,
MC-034 and MC-035, returning "no" early is the valuable outcome.

## Out of scope

- **Scoring the held-out set. Not once, not "just to see".** No
  `Split::HeldOut` selection, no per-file held-out table read or printed, no
  aggregate held-out number, and no threshold chosen, adjusted or rejected on
  anything a held-out entry produced. A spike *is* tuning work — it fits an
  approach and reads per-file results — so every measurement here runs over
  `Split::Tuning`. `corpus.md`'s rule governs: an entry used for any such
  decision is **no longer held out whatever its `split` says**, and
  contamination runs one way. This is the single most important boundary in the
