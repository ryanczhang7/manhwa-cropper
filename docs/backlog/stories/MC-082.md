## Context

[MC-081](MC-081.md) records the user's report of 2026-10-04 and adds seven of
their Eleceed screenshots to `tuning`. On **six** of them the app cropped the
**YouTube window on the right** instead of the manhwa page in the reader's
window on the left (about columns 0..1820):

| MC-081 id | File |
|---|---|
| e01 | `2025-03-16 22_56_00.png` |
| e02 | `2025-03-07 00_20_37.png` |
| e04 | `2025-03-16 22_48_01.png` |
| e05 | `2025-03-16 22_51_37.png` |
| e06 | `2025-03-16 22_51_49.png` |
| e07 | `2025-03-16 22_54_27.png` |

The marks are the user's, frozen in MC-081. MC-081 pins each one's crop on
`main` at both margins as a known exception, exact in both directions; this
story removes those exceptions.

**This is the shape [MC-066](MC-066.md) fixed, and it is back.** MC-066's
`f18` (`2025-03-06 12_48_06.png`) had its crop taken from the second window
(stage 3c's widest textured run was the video), and `f13`
(`2025-03-16 22_47_44.png`, 17 seconds before e04) spanned both windows
(stage 2 peeled the page's left margin). MC-066 shipped one rule per cause:
the page column is the widest run **with a page-margin column beside it**,
and a side strip that is the page's own margin is not chrome. Both still
pass. Why these six do not is **not known**. Candidates, none of them a
finding:
- the right window shows a YouTube **page** (thumbnails, text) rather than
  only a video frame, so a run beside it reads as page margin;
- the reader's window holds less textured art (a gutter, a narrow panel),
  so the page run is narrower or absent over the central band;
- the user's build predates MC-066 (MC-081's RED reproduces each on `main`
  first; a file `main` already crops right leaves this story's scope).

**Before RED, the Lead PO measures** (MC-073 and MC-080's practice), once
MC-081 is DONE, on `main`, with a probe in a scratch worktree outside the
repository:
1. per file, the seam column between the windows (a column whose value
   changes against its left neighbour on more than 1,300 of 1,440 rows, as
   MC-066 measured 1828 and 1820), and the furniture rows `T` (the end of the
   browser bar) and `B` (the start of the reader window's horizontal
   scrollbar or the taskbar);
2. per file, the stage that first goes wrong (`trim_uniform` →
   `content_box` → `trim_within` → `textured_box` → `page_column` →
   `locate`), with the numbers that decide it, beside the same read-out for
   `f13` and `f18`;
3. whether the six share one cause. If they do not, the story splits
   before RED, one story per cause, and the user is told.

`T` and `B` are put to the user with a zoomed picture before RED, as
MC-063's were, and frozen. The criteria below are a **draft**, re-cut once
the cause is measured and recorded under `## Amendments`, as MC-080's were.

**[MC-083](MC-083.md)'s e03** (`2025-03-07 01_02_31.png`) crops the manhwa
but keeps the bookmarks bar and taskbar: a different symptom. It is a
separate story **unless the measurement shows one cause**, in which case the
two merge before RED and the user is told (MC-075 and MC-076's practice).

**Required gate that would fail if the artifact broke:** `integration` for
AC-1 and AC-2, hence `required_gates: [integration]`. AC-3 is in the
required `unit` gate.

## Acceptance criteria

**Draft, to be re-cut after the measurement in `## Context`.** Each
criterion reproduces the bug first: its test is red against today's crop,
which MC-081 pins. "Both margins" means 0 and 3. Column bounds are
end-exclusive. "The six" are the table above; "the seam" is each file's
seam column and `T`/`B` its furniture rows, measured before RED and frozen
by the user.

- **AC-1** — *Each of the six is cropped to the reader's window.* **Given**
  each of the six, **when** it is processed at both margins, **then** the
  outcome is `Cropped` and the crop:
  - contains the user's mark;
  - ends at the seam or left of it, so **no column of the second window**
    is kept;
  - lies within rows `T..B`, so no browser bar, scrollbar or taskbar row is
    kept.

  Whether a `Flagged` outcome (the screenshot left alone) may stand in for a
  crop on any of the six is Open question 2; until answered, it does not
  satisfy this criterion. Cutting the art is never acceptable.
- **AC-2** — *Nothing else moves, and no clip anywhere.*
  - The six are taken off every list MC-081 put them on.
  - Every other marked `tuning` crop is exactly what `main` produces at both
    margins (`corpus_tuning_crops_unmoved.rs`), the six's rows re-measured.
    The split-screen entries already in `tuning` are named in that check and
    must not move: `f13`, `f18`, `2025-03-06 01_22_45.png`,
    `2025-03-07 00_58_06.png`, `n06` `2025-03-13 12_01_01.png` and `n02`
    `2025-03-07 00_05_58.png`.
  - Zero clips holds on every marked `tuning` entry at both margins, except
    MC-083's e03 while it remains listed.
  - `EPIC-08`'s side-edge suites (`corpus_sides.rs`, `corpus_page_column.rs`)
    pass unloosened.
  - **Controls:** the one-column-wider control
    (`widening_every_expected_rect_one_pixel_on_the_columns_makes_the_zero_clip_check_fail`)
    still fires; reverting the fix turns AC-1 red on the six (probe in
    `## Notes`).
- **AC-3** — *A generated reproduction of the measured cause.* A `unit`-gate
  test on a generated image, red against today's code, in which the measured
  cause is switched on (the crop is taken from, or joined to, the second
  window) and, in its **control**, switched off with everything else the
  same (the crop stays in the reader's window). It joins MC-066's
  `crates/core/tests/page_column_second_window.rs` scenes or sits beside
  them. If the shape cannot be built to fail today, `## Notes` says why, with
  the attempt's output, and AC-1 carries the regression alone.

## Model guidance

Model: default. The Lead PO measures before RED and orchestrates; the Test
Developer writes RED; the Feature Developer writes GREEN.

**Oracle partition.**
- **Settled, read out, never re-derived:** the user's marks (MC-081's frozen
  table); `T` and `B` once the user confirms them; zero clips as absolute;
  today's crops (MC-081's pins); MC-066's rulings ("Must crop right", "Stay in
  the reader window") as they apply to `f13` and `f18`.
- **Measured, re-measured in RED and pinned:** each seam column.
- **Oracle-free: the cause.** RED states it as a claim with a control that
  turns it on and off and moves the crop with it (AC-3), never a narrative
  read off one image.
- **Mechanical, pin exactly:** AC-1's containment and bounds, AC-2's list
  removals and unmoved crops.
- **A person's call:** Open questions 1 and 2.

**Success condition, which can come out either way:** after GREEN the six are
`Cropped` inside the reader's window, contain their marks and keep no
furniture row, no other crop moves unlisted, and the integration command with
`--no-fail-fast` is green. It **fails** on a clip anywhere outside MC-083's
listed entry, a crop keeping any column of a second window, any of the six
named split-screen entries moving, a threshold swept until the six pass with
no cause stated and controlled first, or a colour or chroma signal used.

## Open questions

1. **The measured cause, and the furniture rows.** Pending the measurement
   (`## Context`). Put to the user in plain words with the numbers and a
   zoomed picture: what goes wrong, whether one change can fix all six, and
   where the browser bar ends and the taskbar or scrollbar starts on each.
2. **If the app cannot tell which window is the comic, may it leave that
   screenshot alone instead of cropping it?** For `f18` the user answered
   "Must crop right" (MC-066): leaving it alone was allowed only as a
   stopgap, by an approved amendment, with evidence that finding the window
   is not possible. Asked again for these six because they are new
   screenshots; the recommended answer is the same.
