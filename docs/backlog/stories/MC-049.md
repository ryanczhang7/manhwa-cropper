## Context

The user, 2026-09-23: *"The cropping from the side isnt tight enough and I
still see the page on the right and left side."* This reopens the column axis
that `EPIC-07` had closed at 20 of 21, and it overturns
`architecture.md` decision 5's *"a leftover 3 px border is the brief's
accepted cost"* — see [`EPIC-08`](../epics/EPIC-08.md) for why this is a new
epic rather than an amendment of `EPIC-07`.

**What the strip is, measured on 2026-09-23 over the 21 marked `tuning`
entries** (Lead PO probe, outside the repository, reading each column's median
luma and the share of its pixels within `uniform_tolerance` of that median over
the mark's rows; recorded in `## Notes`):

- **The locator is already on the right boundary.** Before `margin::expand`,
  the column rect's edges sit exactly where the columns change from **flat
  page background** (share 1.00) to **textured** (share ≈ 0.1–0.4). On every
  entry checked, *crop gap − 3* equals the number of textured columns outside
  the mark.
- **The visible strip is the margin.** `margin::expand` then adds
  `Tuning::margin_px` = 3 columns on each side, and those three are flat page
  background on every entry — white on a light reader, near-black on a dark
  one. That is the high-contrast band the user sees. 42 of 42 sides carry it.
- **The margin cannot simply go to 0 against today's marks.** Seven entries
  have a crop edge 0–2 px outside the mark, which means the locator is 1–3 px
  *inside* it — and on each of the seven, the mark's own outermost 1–3
  columns are **flat page background** (share 1.00 over every marked row):

  | Entry | Side | flat columns inside the mark |
  |---|---|---|
  | `2025-10-14 23_30_20.png` | right | 3 (all 255) |
  | `Screenshot (2630).jpg` | right | 2 (one at luma 1, one at 11 — see Open question 2) |
  | `Screenshot (67).png` | right | 1 (255) |
  | `Screenshot (70).jpg` | right | 1 (251) |
  | `Screenshot (3187).png` | right | 1 (26) |
  | `2026-01-05 13_33_41.png` | left | 1 (11) |
  | `Screenshot (2708).jpg` | left | 1 (11) |

  Those marks contain page margin against `corpus.md`'s marking rule, which is
  exactly the case MC-027 met on `2026-01-05 13_45_59.png` (five flat columns;
  the user saw the image and **corrected the manifest**, `w` 471 → 466). With
  margin 0 and the marks as they stand, the existing zero-clip test would
  report seven "clips" of nothing but page background.
- **What remains after the margin goes: textured columns outside the mark.** On
  about fifteen entries the page's textured region extends 1–7 columns past
  the mark (17 on `Screenshot (93).jpg`, the diagonal-gutter entry). Its median
  matches the art beside it. Whether the user sees that as "page" is Open
  question 3; this story does not remove it.

The required gate that fails if this story's artifact breaks: `unit`, through
AC-6. The corpus half is `integration`-only, hence `required_gates`.

## Acceptance criteria

A column is **page background** when at least **0.95** of its pixels over the
mark's rows lie within `uniform_tolerance` of that column's median luma.

- **AC-1** — *The bug, reproduced.* Given the 21 marked `tuning` entries, when
  `process_file` runs at `Tuning::default()`, then **no column of the crop that
  lies outside the mark is page background**, on either side. On `cb2deef`
  this fails on **42 of 42 sides** (predicted from the probe; RED confirms the
  count and records it). *Control on the number:* the crop widened by one
  column on one side fails that side on 21 of 21 entries, because the next
  column out is page background on every one. *Control on the metric:* the predicate
  applied to the mark's own first and last columns must call them **not** page
  background on at least 19 of 21 entries after Open question 2's corrections
  (the probe measured art-edge columns at share 0.01–0.73), so a predicate that
  calls everything background is caught as surely as one that calls nothing.
- **AC-2** — *Zero clips, still, against corrected marks.* Given the 21 marked
  `tuning` entries **after the manifest corrections the user approves under
  Open question 2**, when `process_file` runs, then every `Cropped` rect
  contains its mark — **0 clips**. *Control:* the crop narrowed by one column
  on each side clips on **at least 8** entries (predicted: the seven above plus
  `Screenshot (3538).png`'s left, where the locator already sits on the mark
  edge; RED measures). The existing inset-10 controls in `corpus.rs` and
  `corpus_accuracy.rs` stay as they are.
- **AC-3** — *Flags do not move.* The six MC-026 AC-4 pins stay `Flagged` for
  `Detector(NoBorderFound)`; `2025-02-27 22_46_15.png` is reported, not pinned.
- **AC-4** — *The rows do not move beyond the margin change.* Given the 21
  marked entries, each crop's top and bottom edge moves by exactly the change
  in `margin_px` on that side and no more (clamped at the image edge), and
  still contains the mark.
- **AC-5** — *MC-019's column window still holds.* The existing
  `the_left_and_right_edges_of_every_marked_page_land_inside_the_window` and
  `at_least_nine_corpus_screenshots_in_ten_are_right_on_the_column_axis`
  still pass, reading `margin_px` from `Tuning` as they already do. Leftover
  **textured** columns outside the mark are therefore at most 8 on at least
  19 of 21, which the probe predicts as at most 7 on 20 of 21 with
  `Screenshot (93).jpg` the named diagonal exception.
- **AC-6** — *On synthetic images (required `unit` gate).* Given a generated
  page column of textured art on a flat background, when `detect` runs at
  `Tuning::default()`, then the crop's left and right columns are exactly the
  art's first and last columns. Given the art's outermost column just on the
  art side of the rule the locator already applies to call a column page
  margin, it is **kept**; just on the other side, it is **trimmed** — pinning
  the boundary to the existing rule and its settled constant (RED reads which
  rule and which constant out of `trim.rs` / `flat.rs`; it does not invent a
  new one). *Control:* at `margin_px` = 1 the
  first case fails by one column on each side.
- **AC-7** — *The record.* `architecture.md` decision 5 and the `Tuning` table
  carry the new value with the user's words and date; `product-brief.md`
  §4 item 2 carries the amendment; `corpus.md` records each corrected mark
  with its old and new rectangle and the user's ruling, as MC-027 did for
  `13_45_59`.

reaching for a smaller margin. Record the verdict in `## Notes`.

## Open questions — answer before RED

**Answered 2026-09-23: the user accepted every recommended default below, before RED. For MC-049, `Screenshot (2630).jpg`'s 1 px dark column still needs the user's look when the marks are corrected.**

1. **The margin.** Does `margin_px` go to **0 on all four sides**, or to 0 on
   the columns only with the rows keeping 3? *Recommended: 0 on all four.* On
   the columns the margin only ever adds flat page background (measured); on
   the rows it only adds three rows of whatever sits above and below —
   gutter today, and after MC-048 and MC-050 the furniture those stories
   remove — while the row edges sit 97+ px from any art, so it protects
   nothing there. One constant also keeps `Tuning` simple. The cost: decision
   5's safety net is gone, and never-clip on the columns rests on the
   locator's boundary being exact, which is why Open question 4 matters.
2. **The seven marks.** May the manifest be corrected to exclude the flat
   columns listed in `## Context`, on the MC-027 precedent? *Recommended: yes,
   with the images open*, one ruling per entry. One needs a real look:
   **`Screenshot (2630).jpg`**'s inner flat column is uniform at luma **1**
   against a page background of **11** — within `uniform_tolerance`, so the
   locator trims it, but it may be a one-pixel dark panel border that belongs
   to the art. If the user says it is art, that entry keeps its mark, the
   locator clips it by one column at margin 0, and the story stops (the
   success condition above fires).
3. **The textured columns outside the mark.** On ~15 entries the crop will
   still carry 1–7 columns that are textured, not page background, and that
   the mark excludes. Are these what you meant by "the page on the right and
   left side"? *Recommended: look at `2025-10-14 23_29_06.png` (5 left, 7
   right) after this story lands and decide then*; the white or black band is
   by far the more visible artefact, and removing textured columns needs a
   different locator.
4. **Held-out.** Same question as MC-048's Open question 2: one aggregate
   zero-clip run over the 24 marked held-out entries at the end of GATES,
   counts only. *Recommended: yes* — dropping the margin is precisely the
   change that could clip an entry no one has tuned against.
