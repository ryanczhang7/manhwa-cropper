## Context

MC-025 added the flatness signal and its locator, and measured that the signal
is right and the **search rule** is wrong. Its `## Notes` carry the whole
diagnosis; the part that matters here is why 0 of 21 corpus crop entries moved.

`edges::textured_span` returns the **outermost** indices of a spread profile at
or above `Tuning::min_line_spread`. On a real screenshot there is textured
content all the way out to the image edge - browser UI, a second window, the
taskbar - so the outermost textured column is column 0 and the span is the
whole rect. Measured on the rect `detect` hands `flat::textured_box`, on four
corpus files, the span is `columns (0, 2544)` and `rows (4, 1351)` every time.
Alternating the axes does not break the deadlock either: it reaches a fixed
point on the first or second pass, still spanning the full width.

The signal underneath it is not in doubt. Columns measured over the marked
rect's rows locate the page's left and right edges within a few pixels, and on
the left always on the safe side:

```
file                      located x     marked x       left off   right off
Screenshot (3187).png       984..1560     987..1561          -3          -1
Screenshot (2744).jpg       948..1596     950..1589          -2          +7
2025-10-20 15_37_25.png    1006..1539    1008..1536          -2          +3
Screenshot (2630).jpg       955..1591     958..1593          -3          -2
Screenshot (2582).jpg      1006..1539    1008..1532          -2          +7
Screenshot (1661).png      1073..2556    1077..1470          -4       +1086
```

So the question this story answers is not "is this line flat" - MC-025 settled
that - but **"where is the flat *pair* that brackets the page"**. A reader page
is an interior band: a run of textured lines with a run of flat lines on each
side of it, somewhere inside a screenshot whose outer regions are textured.
Finding the widest such band, or the one nearest the image's centre, is a
different search over the same profile, and it is the one that has to be
designed and calibrated here.

`Screenshot (1661).png` is in the table deliberately: its right edge is off by
1086, because a second textured region sits to the right of the page. Whatever
rule this story picks has to say something defensible about that file, and it
is the reason "widest band" may not be the right rule on its own.

Gate that fails if this breaks: `unit`.

## Acceptance criteria

To be written when this story is planned. The shape is known and the numbers
are not: a search over a spread profile that returns an interior band rather
than the outermost textured indices; the rule stated as a testable property;
a synthetic fixture with textured regions on **both** sides of the page, which
is the case MC-025's fade fixture deliberately does not have; the corpus
measurement before and after, as MC-025 AC-6 did it; and every existing
assertion still holding, MC-025's seventeen included.

## Model guidance

Read MC-025's `## Notes` in full before planning this. It carries the three
measurements, the harness that took them, and the reason the premise in
MC-025's own `## Context` did not carry to the corpus - a measurement of
*gutter inside the marked rect* is not evidence that the boundary can be
**found** from the outside, and that distinction is the whole of this story.

Settled and not to be re-derived: `min_line_spread` 8.0 and everything
`Tuning::default()` holds. Retuning stays MC-019's.

## Out of scope

Retuning any existing constant (MC-019). Meeting the 90% bar (MC-019). Colour
or chroma signals - measured and ruled out in MC-025's `## Context`. Changing
the corpus or its manifest.
