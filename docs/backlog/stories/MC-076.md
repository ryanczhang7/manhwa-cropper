## Context

[MC-071](MC-071.md)'s held-out run (scored commit `4539e12`) kept 93 rows of
browser chrome on `n13`, `Screenshot (2507).png` (`demonicrevolution`, dark,
2560x1440), the held-out set's only `demonicrevolution` entry:

- crop at margin 0: columns 977..1577 (exact), rows **40..1392** (end
  exclusive);
- the user's mark: `977,133 600x1259`, rows 133..1392;
- MC-071's frozen oracle: `T` 133, `B` 1392. The bottom is exact; only the
  top is wrong, by 93 rows.

[MC-072](MC-072.md) moves it to `tuning` and records whether the viewport
stage declines on it.

**Why this is its own story, for now.** [MC-075](MC-075.md)'s two crops keep
the **whole** height, chrome and taskbar both. This one starts at row 40, not
0, and stops exactly at the taskbar: something removed the top 40 rows and
the taskbar but not the toolbar between rows 40 and 133. Another reader,
another browser-bar height (133, not 115), and a different shape of failure.
If the measurement shows one cause, the two stories **merge** before RED (the
user told); otherwise they stay apart.

**Nothing below is a finding.** No agent has measured this image.
Candidates:

- **Candidate A: the stage declines**, and a later stage (`content_box`'s
  chrome peel) removes only the tab strip (rows 0..40) and the taskbar,
  leaving the toolbar as if it were page.
- **Candidate B: the stage locates a viewport whose top is 40**, on a strong
  row line at the tab strip's end rather than the toolbar's.
- **Evidence that the layout alone is not the cause:** `tuning` holds three
  `demonicrevolution` screenshots with the same bars, `Screenshot (1720).png`,
  `Screenshot (2461).png` and `Screenshot (2486).png`, whose viewport the
  stage locates at **133..1392** (`STAGE_MEASURED`). So what fails is in
  `n13`'s content near row 133, not where its bars are.

**Before RED, the Lead PO measures:** `viewport::locate` beside the
pipeline's page column, the row profile over the page column across rows
0..200, the strong row lines there, and `content_box`'s removed strips; and,
for comparison, the same rows on (2461) and (2486).

### Measured (2026-10-02, before RED, on `main` at `140f0fd`)

The Lead PO measured with a probe in a scratch worktree outside the
repository (the shipped crates, `viewport`'s helpers made public, a test
printing per-row and per-column shares). Tolerance 10.

**Candidate A, with a different cause than the draft guessed.** On all four
`demonicrevolution` screenshots the earlier stages agree:
`trim_uniform` `0,4 2560x1432`, `content_box` peels `[Top, Bottom, Bottom]`
to `0,40 2560x1352` (the tab strip and the taskbar), and the page column is
exact. So the crop's row 40 is the chrome peel's, and the toolbar between 40
and 133 is left because the viewport stage **declines** on `n13`
(`locate` `None`) and locates 133..1392 on the other three.

What differs is **not** near row 133. Each page sits between two flat
**reader panels** of another tone (medians 34 and 42 against the site tone
22, so 0.00 within tolerance), hugging the page column on both sides, with
site background beyond them:

| Entry | Page column | Left panel | Right panel | Whole-margin share, viewport rows | `locate` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `n13` (2507) | 977..1577 (600) | 872..976 (105) | 1577..1681 (105) | **0.889** | `None` |
| (2461) | 1010..1544 (534) | 917..1009 (93) | 1544..1636 (93) | 0.905 | 133..1392 |
| (1720) | 1010..1544 (534) | 917..1009 (93) | 1544..1636 (93) | 0.905 | 133..1392 |
| (2486) | 1078..1478 (400) | 1008..1077 (70) | 1478..1547 (70) | 0.933 | 133..1392 |

The share is the panels' (and the scrollbar's) width over the margin's,
constant on every viewport row: n13's (210 + 7) / 1960 off gives 0.889.
The draft's "evidence that the layout alone is not the cause" was wrong:
the bar heights are the same, but the panel width relative to the page
is the layout, and it alone decides. Rows 0..130 read 0.000 to 0.010 on
all four. `n13` and MC-075's `n06` are different causes (MC-075 `## Context`).

**The two `2025-08-05` WebPs have the same shape.** Tone 35. `00_11_13`:
page column 953..1593, a flat panel (medians 23 and 24, 12 levels off the
tone) at 819..951 and on the right beyond 1593, whole-margin share **0.862**
on viewport rows. `00_11_27`: 1006..1539, panel 896..1003, share **0.894**.
Between page and panel each has a sliver of page-tone columns (MC-052: 0 to
4; `00_11_13`'s column 952 is within tolerance on 0.52 of its rows). MC-048
AC-3 (user-approved, 2026-09-23) pins both WebPs' crops exactly as they are
and names chrome removal on them a limitation. No threshold separates
`n13`'s 0.889 from the WebPs' 0.862 and 0.894.

**Two trial rules** (scratch worktree only, full `--include-ignored` run;
both run only after the whole-margin reading and MC-075's fallback decline,
then re-read the whole margin with the panels left out, MC-054's chrome
check after):
1. *Leave out a band of non-background columns hugging the page.* `n13`
   comes out **exactly its mark**, `977,133 600x1259` (m0) and
   `974,133 606x1259` (m3). The WebPs do not move, but only because the
   page-tone sliver stops the band before it starts, on `00_11_13` a column
   that is page tone on 52% of its rows: a coin flip, not a rule. A
   generated case with a band on one side only (147x110, band 135..147)
   fails `detect.rs`'s `the_margin_is_read_from_the_tuning_it_was_given`
   and `viewport.rs`'s `the_stage_reads_uniform_tolerance_from_the_tuning`.
2. *The same, needing a band on both sides, with a sliver of up to 4
   page-tone columns absorbed.* `n13` is exact again, and **both WebPs
   move**: rows **115..1400** (`953,115 640x1285`, `1006,115 533x1285` at
   m0), dropping the browser chrome. `00_11_27` keeps its mark;
   `00_11_13`'s mark starts at row **114**, so it **clips by one row**.
   Every other crop unmoved, the generator passes; `viewport.rs`'s
   `the_stage_reads_uniform_tolerance_from_the_tuning` still fails.

**Row 114 on `00_11_13.webp`.** Zoomed (rows 100..130, columns 930..1130,
x8): row 114 is the dark one-pixel line at the bottom of the browser
toolbar, the same tone across page and margin; the art starts at row 115.
MC-048 recorded this as the defect that made its original AC-3
unsatisfiable.

**The decision this needs** (put to the user before RED): fix `n13` alone
on a rule the WebPs escape by one column; or treat all three alike, which
reverses MC-048 AC-3's ruling and needs `00_11_13`'s mark top ruled
browser bar (114 → 115); or leave `n13` a named limitation, as the WebPs are.
